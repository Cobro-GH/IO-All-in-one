職責Duties

據官方：入境事務主任主要執行與入境、人事登記、生死及婚姻登記等香港法例有關的職務，包括一般行政及督導工作，當中包括但不限於偵查偽造證件。

According to the official page, an Immigration Officer mainly carries out duties under Hong Kong law on immigration control, registration of persons, and births, deaths and marriages, including general administration and supervision, and including but not limited to detecting forged documents.

註：須受紀律約束、接受全日制入職訓練、穿著制服、輪班當值，並或須在香港特別行政區以外的地方工作。

Note: the post is subject to discipline, full-time induction training, wearing uniform and shift duty, and may require work outside the Hong Kong SAR.

薪酬（招聘頁所示，或會更改）Pay (as shown on the recruitment page; it may change)

紀律人員（主任級）薪級表第 7 點至第 22 點：約每月港幣 46,630 元至 85,515 元（頂薪僅供參考）。

Disciplined Services (Officer) Pay Scale Point 7 to Point 22: about HK$46,630 to HK$85,515 a month (the maximum is for reference only).

  • 學士／文憑／高級文憑／副學士等＋中英達標：約 49,300 元A bachelor’s degree, diploma, higher diploma, associate degree or similar, plus the Chinese and English standard: about $49,300

You must meet the academic and language requirements, pass the fitness test and the national security law test, and be able to speak fluent Cantonese and English, among other conditions. 聘用與入職訓練Appointment and induction training

  • 按公務員試用條款，試用期 三年。Civil-service probationary terms: a probationary period of three years.
  • 新聘主任須在入境事務學院接受約 二十五星期留宿入職訓練。A newly appointed officer must complete about twenty-five weeks of residential induction training at the department’s training institute (入境事務學院).
  • 獲取錄後須通過職前體格檢查（包括色覺測試）。After selection you must pass a pre-employment medical examination, including a colour-vision test.
  • 申請一般經公務員事務局 G.F. 340 網上系統。Applications are normally made through the Civil Service Bureau’s G.F. 340 online system.

常見混淆：助理員／海關Common mix-ups: Assistant / Customs

  • 入境處對人（出入境管制、簽證／許可、人事登記、生死婚姻登記等）；海關對貨（貨品、稅項、走私等）——面試常考，勿說錯。ImmD deals with people (immigration control, visas and permits, registration of persons, births, deaths and marriages). Customs deals with goods (cargo, duty, smuggling). This is often tested. Do not mix them up.
  • 入境事務助理員另有全年招聘；甄選多為體能→小組討論→甄選面試，一般無主任級那套筆試（以官方助理員頁為準）。Immigration Assistants have a separate year-round recruitment. Selection is often fitness, then group discussion, then a selection interview, and it generally does not include the officer-grade written test (follow the official Assistant page).
  • 助理員小組討論官方 PDF 指以廣東話進行。主任初步面試按本站最新說明練習：中英各一題、考官給題、無素材、約 10–14 人、每題通常 1–2 次發言，接着是零準備的 2:30 英語演講。見小組討論硬卡和演講時鐘。The official Assistant group-discussion PDF says the discussion is in Cantonese. For the officer initial interview, train the latest brief on this site: one Chinese topic and one English topic, set by the panel, no reading pack, about 10–14 candidates, usually one or two turns per topic, then a 2:30 English talk with no preparation. See the discussion card and the speech clock.
