整體備考計劃（4–8 週）A 4–8 week plan

按你距離初步面試／筆試／最後面試的遠近剪裁。體能需長期操，勿只溫書。

Cut this to how far you are from the initial interview, the written test or the final interview. Fitness needs steady practice. Do not only revise notes.

原則Principles

  • 先對準下一關，同時保底下一關所需的語言肌肉。Aim at the next stage, and keep the language practice the stage after that will need.
  • 每週至少一次「全程模擬」（計時＋自評表）。At least one full timed simulation a week, with a scorecard.
  • 時事採「卡片化」：定義／影響／與 ImmD 關係／你的建議。Turn current affairs into cards: definition, impact, the link to ImmD, and your suggestion.
  • Drive「IO」筆記併入後，把個人錯題優先生進每日複習。After the Drive “IO” notes are folded in, put your own mistakes first in the daily review.

4 週速成（下一關＝初步面試）A 4-week sprint (next stage = initial interview)

週Week 重點Focus 每日建議Daily suggestion
第 1 週Week 1 形式弄清＋粵／英討論骨架；PREP 開場Get the format clear, plus Cantonese and English discussion frames; open with PREP 粵語題一次發言（建基→延伸→昇華）＋演講詞 1 個、零準備 2:30；先讀考前一屏One Cantonese turn (build → extend → lift) and one speech word, 2:30 with no preparation; read the one-screen sheet first
第 2 週Week 2 英語討論流暢度；陌生字演講Fluency in English discussion; a speech on an unfamiliar word 英語題一次發言＋演講 2 個詞（仍是零準備 2:30）；聽填充詞和收句是否在 2:15–2:30One English turn and two speech words, still 2:30 with no preparation; listen for fillers and whether you finished between 2:15 and 2:30
第 3 週Week 3 雙語交替模擬；衝突句式Alternate the two languages; conflict phrases 中英各一題的討論（每題只准自己講 1–2 次）＋即席 2:30、零準備，每日 3 次；套裝、坐姿、眼神、聲線One Chinese topic and one English topic, with only one or two turns of your own on each, plus a 2:30 talk with no preparation, three times a day; suit, posture, eyes, voice
第 4 週Week 4 減量保質；錯題重練Do less and keep the quality; redo mistakes 隔日全模擬；休息與睡眠優先A full simulation every other day; rest and sleep come first

8 週穩打（含筆試＋面試底子）An 8-week build (written test and interview base)

體能（與筆試／面試並行）Fitness (alongside the written test and interview)

  • 800 米：每週 2–3 次間歇＋1 次長慢跑800 metres: interval work two or three times a week, plus one longer easy run
  • 仰臥起坐／蹲撐立：隔日練，留意官方姿勢Sit-ups and squat thrusts: every other day, and follow the official form
  • 立定跳遠、來回跑：每週技術課 1–2 次Standing long jump and shuttle run: one or two technique sessions a week
  • 測驗前一週減量，避免受傷Taper in the week before the test so you do not get injured

一週時間盒示例（平日 90 分鐘）A sample weekday box (90 minutes)

  1. 20 分：時事／部門網20 minutes: current affairs or the department site
  2. 30 分：GD 或面試口述30 minutes: group discussion or interview speaking
  3. 25 分：演講或筆試題25 minutes: a speech or a written-test item
  4. 15 分：自評表＋錯題本15 minutes: the scorecard and the error log
