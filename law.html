法例答題公式（強 vs 弱）How to answer a law question

弱：「我可以拘捕。」Weak: “I can arrest.”

強：「必須有合理懷疑，按法定目的行事，遵守時間限制及職級要求，保障涉事人士權利（如同性搜查、非公開），並按指揮鏈匯報。」Strong: “There must be reasonable suspicion. Act for the statutory purpose, keep to the time limit and the rank, protect the person’s rights (same-sex search, not in public), and report up the chain of command.”

Cap.331 前線權力口訣（拆開說）Cap. 331 front-line powers, step by step

Drive 口訣是一條鏈，不是一句口號。Part I 罪行的具體範圍以條例附表為準，下面不另造清單。

  1. 先有合理懷疑。懷疑的是 Part I 罪行，不是「看起來不合作」。沒有這個前提，後面的截停、羈留和搜查都沒有起點。
  2. 截停並查身份。目的是確認這個人是誰，不是先定他有罪。
  3. 為查問而羈留。筆記把 s.12 記成不超過 12 小時，由事務隊成員行使。時限一到就要放行或轉到另一項法定權力，不能用口頭延長。
  4. 搜查。合理而且必要；由同性別人員進行。對方反對公開搜查，就不要在公眾視線下做。答題時權力後面一定跟這句保障。
  5. 逮捕。只有在逮捕的法定理由成立時才做。弱答是「我可以拘捕」；強答是合理懷疑、法定目的、時限、職級、權利、匯報。
  6. 逮捕之後。帶往入境事務處辦事處或警署，不是留在櫃檯繼續理論。
  7. 保釋。筆記記保釋職級是 IO 或以上。記不住當日職級時，說「按條例指定職級」，不要猜。
  8. 破門、手令、拍照和指紋。各自有法定條件，不是逮捕的自動附帶。用到才引用，用不到不要堆。

The Drive mnemonic is a chain, not a slogan. The exact scope of a Part I offence follows the schedule to the ordinance. No extra list is invented below.

  1. Reasonable suspicion first. The suspicion is of a Part I offence, not “the person looks uncooperative”. Without that starting point, a later stop, detention or search has no basis.
  2. Stop and check identity. The purpose is to confirm who the person is, not to convict them first.
  3. Detain for inquiry. The notes record s. 12 as not more than 12 hours, exercised by a member of the Service. When the limit is reached the person must be released or moved to another statutory power. It cannot be extended by word of mouth.
  4. Search. Reasonable and necessary, by an officer of the same sex. If the person objects to a search in public, do not do it in public view. In an answer, the safeguard follows the power.
  5. Arrest. Only when the statutory grounds for arrest are made out. The weak answer is “I can arrest”. The strong answer is reasonable suspicion, statutory purpose, time limit, rank, rights and a report.
  6. After arrest. Take the person to an Immigration Department office or a police station. Do not keep arguing at the counter.
  7. Bail. The notes record the rank for bail as IO or above. If you cannot remember the rank that day, say “the rank specified in the ordinance”. Do not guess.
  8. Forced entry, warrant, photograph and fingerprints. Each has its own statutory condition. They are not automatic extras of an arrest. Cite one only when you use it.

Cap.115 高頻罪名類（勿逐條罰則）Cap. 115 offence types that come up often

面試引用方式：說出條次在管哪一種行為、一個典型場面、然後聲明罰則以電子法例為準。僱主「罰款 50 萬港元及監禁 10 年」是角度頁已有的非法受僱僱主刑罰，不要自行安到下面每一條。

  • s.38 無許可入境或逗留。場面：沒有有效許可卻入境，或許可到期後仍留下。答法：「我先核對他有沒有許可、許可到何時；無許可逗留才考慮 s.38，不要把仍在期限內的訪客說成這一條。」
  • s.38AA 禁止就業、開業或就讀。場面：訪客在港開工、開店或註冊入學。答法：先讀落地條件是不是 Visitor，再說明這一條針對的是「沒有獲准就去做這些活動」，與僱主責任分開講。
  • s.38A 地盤非法勞工及地盤主管責任。場面：建築地盤發現不能合法受僱的人，責任不止在工人，也在地盤主管有沒有採取預防措施。答法：用它說明「僱主／主管責任」，不要只描述一次拘捕。
  • s.41 違反逗留條件。場面：學生超時工作、外傭為另一僱主打工、一般就業未獲准就換公司。答法：拿出該張落地條件的限制，說明違反的是條件本身；13 類條件的解釋在證件頁。
  • s.42 虛假陳述、偽造文書一類。場面：假學歷、假聘書、假簽證頁，或在申請表上作不實陳述。答法：接S4和假學歷新聞，強調文件真偽與人員判斷，不背刑罰格。
  • s.43 違反遞解令。場面：已被遞解離境的人再次違反該命令入境或逗留。答法：先確認存在遞解令，再講這一條；不要把普通逾期逗留或遣送都叫成遞解。

How to cite in an interview: say what conduct the section covers, give one typical scene, then say the penalty follows the e-Legislation. The employer penalty of a fine of HK$500,000 and 10 years’ imprisonment is the illegal-employment figure already on the angles page. Do not attach it to every section below.

  • s. 38 — landing or remaining without permission. Scene: entry without a valid permission, or remaining after it expires. Answer: “I first check whether there is permission and until when. I consider s. 38 only if there is no permission to stay. I do not use this section for a visitor who is still within the limit.”
  • s. 38AA — employment, business or study prohibited. Scene: a visitor starts work, opens a business or registers as a student in Hong Kong. Answer: first read whether the landing condition is Visitor, then say this section is about doing those activities without permission. Keep it separate from the employer’s liability.
  • s. 38A — illegal workers on a construction site, and the site controller’s duty. Scene: someone who cannot lawfully be employed is found on a construction site. Liability is not only the worker’s; it includes whether the site controller took preventive measures. Use it to explain employer / controller responsibility, not only one arrest.
  • s. 41 — breach of a condition of stay. Scene: a student works beyond the limit, a domestic helper works for another employer, or a person on general employment changes company without permission. Answer: take the limit on that landing condition and say the breach is of the condition itself. The 13 classes are explained on the identity page.
  • s. 42 — false statements, forged documents and the like. Scene: fake qualifications, a fake contract, a fake visa page, or a false statement on an application. Answer: join S4 and the fake-credential news. Stress document authenticity and an officer’s judgement. Do not recite the penalty grid.
  • s. 43 — breach of a deportation order. Scene: a person who has been deported enters or remains again in breach of that order. Answer: first confirm that a deportation order exists, then cite this section. Do not call an ordinary overstay, or a removal, a deportation.

HD Flashcards

DPP 1–6　保障資料原則DPP 1–6 — data protection principles

《個人資料（私隱）條例》的六項原則，筆記收成：收集目的與方式、準確及保留期限、使用、保安、公開透明、查閱及更正。入境場景是旅客資料、人面辨識、申請表和內部轉交。

應考一句。「我們為核實身份而收集必須的資料，只按原定目的使用，限制查閱的人，並讓當事人在法律允許下查閱和更正。」不要回答「部門可以看全部資料」。

The six principles in the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, compressed in the notes: purpose and manner of collection, accuracy and retention, use, security, openness, and access and correction. Immigration scenes are passenger data, facial recognition, application forms and internal transfer.

Exam line. “We collect what is necessary to verify identity, use it only for that purpose, limit who can see it, and let the person access and correct it where the law allows.” Do not answer “the department can see all the data”.

《基本法》第八條Basic Law Article 8

香港原有法律，除與《基本法》相抵觸或經立法機關修訂者外，予以保留。普通法、衡平法、條例、附屬立法和習慣法，都在這句的範圍裡，而不是 1997 年之後才從零開始。

應考一句。用它說明 Cap.115、Cap.331 為何仍然是日常執法的依據，然後立刻回到「以現行電子法例文本為準」，不要停在憲法口號。

The laws previously in force in Hong Kong are maintained, except for any that contravene the Basic Law or are amended by the legislature. Common law, equity, ordinances, subsidiary legislation and customary law sit inside that sentence. They did not start from zero after 1997.

Exam line. Use it to explain why Cap. 115 and Cap. 331 are still the basis of daily enforcement, then return at once to “the current e-Legislation text prevails”. Do not stop at a constitutional slogan.

Actus reus／Mens reaActus reus / mens rea

Actus reus 是罪行要求的行為或狀態，例如無許可而逗留、作出虛假陳述。Mens rea 是該罪行要求的心理狀態，例如明知或故意；它不是個人動機。動機可以解釋為什麼這樣做，但不能代替條文要求的意圖。

應考一句。「我先指出行為是否符合條文，再看條文要不要證明他明知。同情或經濟壓力是酌情背景，不是自動的免責。」

Actus reus is the conduct or state the offence requires, for example remaining without permission, or making a false statement. Mens rea is the mental state that offence requires, for example knowledge or intention. It is not the person’s motive. Motive can explain why they did it. It cannot replace the intention the section requires.

Exam line. “I first say whether the conduct fits the section, then whether the section requires proof that the person knew. Sympathy or financial pressure is background for discretion. It is not an automatic defence.”

庭審次序Order of testimony

Examination-in-chief 由傳召證人的一方先問，建立案情，一般不誘導。Cross-examination 由對方質問，可以質疑可信性。Re-examination 只澄清盤問中出現的事項，不能借機開新題。

應考一句。如果被問到作供，說「我只講自己經手的事實和記錄，不在主問時加推測；盤問時回答所問，覆問時不補充新故事。」

Examination-in-chief is asked first by the party who called the witness, to build the case, and is generally not leading. Cross-examination is asked by the other side and may test credibility. Re-examination only clarifies what arose in cross-examination. It is not a chance to open a new topic.

Exam line. If asked about giving evidence, say: “I speak only to facts and records I handled. I do not add speculation in chief. In cross-examination I answer what is asked. In re-examination I do not add a new story.”
