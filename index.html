考核四環（內容準備順序）Four stages (order for content, not the official sequence)

  • 筆試 Paper I＋IIWritten Papers I and II
    官方：卷一 90 題／1.5h；卷二英≥500＋中≥600／2h。技巧見 Drive MCQ Hacks；作文見 Angles。Official: Paper I, 90 items / 1.5h; Paper II, English ≥500 and Chinese ≥600 / 2h. Shortcuts are in the Drive MCQ hacks; essays use the angles.
  • 小組討論Group discussion
    中文一題＋英文一題，考官直接給題，無閱讀素材。約 10–14 人，自由發言，考官只觀察。每題通常 1–2 次發言。考前一屏見討論頁。One Chinese topic and one English topic, given by the panel, with no reading pack. About 10–14 people, free speech, examiners only observe. Usually one or two turns per topic. The one-screen sheet is on the discussion page.
  • 即席演講Impromptu talk
    全英語，抽一個抽象英文詞，零準備，約 2 分 30 秒。Drive 的 12 秒準備＋3:00 只是舊練習。時鐘見演講頁。All in English, one abstract word, no preparation, about 2 minutes 30 seconds. Drive’s 12-second preparation plus 3:00 is an old drill only. The clock is on the speech page.
  • 最後面試Final interview
    職能地圖、法例時限、證件邏輯、情景 GSMEAC。Service map, time limits, document logic, and a GSMEAC scenario.

官方甄選順序（主任）常為：體能 → 初步面試（討論＋演講）→ 筆試 → 最後面試。本站「內容章節」按 BRIEF 以筆試技巧與角度庫為 P0；請同時看入職條件。The official officer sequence is often: fitness → initial interview (discussion and talk) → written test → final interview. This site’s chapters follow the brief and put written shortcuts and the angle bank first. Also read the entry requirements.
