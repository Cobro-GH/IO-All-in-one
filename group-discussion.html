考場形式（硬卡）Exam format (hard card)

按本站最新備考說明練習，不要再練舊制。招聘頁未必逐分鐘刊登。當日邀請信如果寫了不同指示，以當日為準。下面這張卡是現在要練的形式。Practise the format in the latest brief on this site. Do not keep drilling the old format. The recruitment page may not publish every minute. If the invitation letter on the day says otherwise, follow the day. This card is the format to train now.

  • 中文一題＋英文一題。廣東話討論一條，英語討論另一條。不是兩條都用同一種語言。
  • 題目由考官直接給。沒有考生自擬題目，沒有先協商出題，沒有閱讀素材包。
  • 約 10–14 人一組，自由發言。沒有指定你必須當主席，也沒有輪流稿。
  • 考官只觀察。他們不加入討論、不提示、不主持。你是在對組員說，同時讓考官聽見。
  • 每條題通常只有 1–2 次發言。這是這節最要緊的限制。開口少，所以一次要聽完別人、推進討論，並留下主任級的判斷。不要儲着等第三次。
  • 討論完隨即是全英語即席演講：抽一個抽象英文詞，零準備，約 2 分 30 秒。
  • One Chinese topic and one English topic. One discussion in Cantonese, the other in English. Not two topics in the same language.
  • The examiners give the topics. Candidates do not design them, do not negotiate a question, and do not receive a reading pack.
  • About 10–14 candidates speak freely. Nobody is appointed chair, and there is no scripted round.
  • Examiners only observe. They do not join, prompt or chair. You speak to the group, and the panel hears you.
  • Usually only one or two turns on each topic. This is the constraint that matters. You speak rarely, so one turn must show that you heard the others, move the discussion on, and leave an officer-level judgement. Do not save yourself for a third turn.
  • The impromptu talk follows at once: one abstract English word, no preparation, about 2 minutes 30 seconds, entirely in English.

評分重點／考官看什麼What the panel is described as watching

綜合 JobMarket 訪問入境處訓練人員及坊間整理，考官較常觀察：From a JobMarket interview with ImmD training staff, and from public summaries, panels are often described as watching:

  • 理解與分析：能否抓住議題核心、分持份者、提可行建議
  • 溝通技巧：清晰、有結構、用詞得體（紀律部隊語境）
  • 聆聽與互動：有否消化他人觀點再發言；避免重複或斷章取義
  • 團隊貢獻：推進共識、協調分歧、控時，而非獨佔發言
  • 態度：尊重、冷靜、有主見但不攻擊人身
  • Understanding and analysis: can you catch the core, separate stakeholders, and offer a workable suggestion
  • Communication: clear, structured, and appropriate to a disciplined service
  • Listening: do you take in another view before you speak, rather than repeat it or quote it out of context
  • Contribution to the group: move toward a consensus, handle disagreement, watch the time — not monopolise the floor
  • Manner: respect, calm, a view of your own without a personal attack

準備策略與時間表建議How to prepare

  1. 每週 2–3 次模擬：找同伴或自錄；粵語、英語各練。
  2. 建立「政策／時事卡片」：定義 → 現況 → 持份者 → 利弊 → 建議（各 30 秒可講完）。
  3. 練習「一句定調＋兩點展開＋一句收束」的 45–90 秒發言。
  4. 錄影檢查：語氣、眼神（若面對面）、有否搶話、有否只顧自己。
  5. 同時準備運動服＋面試正裝（若體能與面試安排接近）。
  1. Two or three times a week: practise with a partner or record yourself, once in Cantonese and once in English.
  2. Build policy / current-affairs cards: definition → what is happening → stakeholders → pros and cons → a suggestion (each speakable in about 30 seconds).
  3. Practise a 45–90 second turn: one line to set the stance, two points, one line to close.
  4. Watch a recording: tone, eye contact if you are face to face, whether you cut in, whether you only follow your own thread.
  5. Also prepare sports kit and interview dress if the fitness test and the interview sit close together.

技巧清單與常見錯誤Skills and common mistakes

技巧Skills

  • 開場可提議流程：「先定義關鍵詞 → 各抒己見 → 比較方案 → 總結共識」。
  • 用建基→延伸→昇華：先確認上一點，再補盲點，再給一項具體政策。不要停在「我補充」。
  • 提出可執行建議（短期／中期、資源、風險）。
  • 意見不合時：重述對方論點 → 指出分歧點 → 提折衷或驗證標準。
  • 英語討論：短句、主動語態、少用填充詞；關鍵詞提前講。
  • You may open by proposing a process: define the key word, hear views, compare options, summarise what is agreed.
  • Use build → extend → lift: confirm the last point, name a blind spot, then one concrete policy. Do not stop at “I would add”.
  • Offer a suggestion that can be carried out (short term / medium term, resources, risk).
  • When you disagree: restate the other point, name the difference, then offer a compromise or a test.
  • In English: short sentences, active voice, few fillers, and the key word early.

  • 搶著講、打斷、冷嘲熱諷
  • 長篇無重點；或只重複他人金句
  • 完全無立場，或情緒化「網民式」宣洩

發言公式：建基 → 延伸 → 昇華Turn formula: build → extend → lift

每次開口只走這三步。發言機會通常只有 1–2 次，所以三步要在同一輪講完。

  1. 建基。用一句確認剛才那一點，點名是誰的觀點。讓考官聽見你有聽。
  2. 延伸。指出一個盲點或未講的持份者，而不是重複金句。
  3. 昇華。落到一項具體、行政上做得到的政策。要同時看見法例／國家安全底線、行政可行性，以及旅客體驗和邊境保安誰付代價。

Every turn uses only these three steps. You usually speak once or twice, so finish all three in the same turn.

  1. Build. One sentence that confirms the point just made, and names whose point it was. The panel hears that you listened.
  2. Extend. Name a blind spot or a stakeholder who has not been mentioned. Do not repeat the line.
  3. Lift. Land on one concrete policy that administration can actually run. Hold the legal and national-security floor, administrative feasibility, and who pays between traveller experience and border security.
示例（科技效率 → 後備／私隱 → 合作查驗）Sample (tech speed → backup / privacy → joint inspection)

「建基你講科技可以加快合資格旅客。我想延伸一個盲點：系統停了、私隱，以及識別不到的人，一定要有後備和人工櫃檯。昇華到政策：皇崗的合作查驗、一次放行，是合資格的人少排一次隊。檢查還在。」

“Building on your point that technology speeds eligible travellers: the blind spot is downtime, privacy, and people the system cannot identify. We need a backup and a staffed counter. The policy step is joint inspection and one release at Huanggang: eligible people queue once. The check stays.”

反面：不要停在加強宣傳、加人手、加罰則Anti-pattern: do not stop at publicity, more staff, or heavier penalties

加強宣傳、加人手、加罰則是表面建議。三句都可以說，但說完還沒有主任的大局。考官聽的是你會不會把題目抬高一層：

  • 法例和國家安全是底線，不能用方便把它談掉。見一屏的第 23 條和 Cap.115。
  • 行政可行性：系統、紀錄、職級、時限、跨部門誰負責。加人手如果沒有流程，只是更長的櫃檯。
  • 持份者：旅客體驗和邊境保安往往是同一條隊伍的兩面。快，是給合資格的人；有疑問的人仍然停下。

More publicity, more staff, heavier penalties are surface suggestions. You may say them, but they are not yet an officer’s wider view. The panel is listening for whether you lift the topic:

  • Law and national security are the floor. Convenience does not talk them away. See Article 23 and Cap. 115 on the one-screen sheet.
  • Administrative feasibility: systems, records, rank, time limits, and which department owns the step. More staff without a process is only a longer counter.
  • Stakeholders: traveller experience and border security are often two sides of the same queue. Speed is for eligible people. A doubtful case still stops.

角色：造球者／統合者Roles: setter and integrator

約 10–14 人裡，你不會每分鐘都說話。兩種有用的位置：造球者拋一個別人接得住的點，並邀請未發言的人；統合者在討論卡死時拉回原則。其餘角色是補位，不是頭銜。In a group of about 10–14 you will not speak every minute. Two useful places: the setter offers a point others can take up, and invites someone who has been quiet; the integrator pulls the discussion back to a principle when it sticks. Other roles fill a gap. They are not titles.

衝突處理句式（粵）

邀請未發言的人（造球）。「你未出聲。我想聽你點睇：旅客體驗同邊境保安，邊一面先受損？」

卡死時拉回（統合）。「我哋卡喺加人手。拉返去：法例同國家安全係底線；行政上做不做得到，先算方案。」

衝突。「兩位嘅出發點我都理解：一邊重視效率，一邊重視把關。不如我哋定一個標準——例如在不降低保安水平下，邊啲措施可以先做？」

Conflict bridge (EN)

Invite a quiet member (setter). “You have not spoken yet. I want your view: which gives way first, the traveller’s experience or border security?”

Pull back when stuck (integrator). “We are stuck on adding staff. Back to the principle: the law and national security are the floor. A proposal counts only if administration can run it.”

Conflict. “I hear both sides — efficiency and the check. Can we set one test: nothing that weakens security, then rank what administration can actually do?”

利弊起手（可選）Pro / con starters (optional)

考官給的題如果是「應否」，用一項利、一項弊、一句主任判斷。不要兩邊各背三點。事實仍以一屏的連結為準。If the given topic is a yes/no, use one benefit, one cost, and one officer judgement. Do not memorise three points on each side. Facts still follow the links on the one-screen sheet.

24 小時通關（皇崗旅客和客運車輛）。利：少了「趕在關門前」的人龍。弊：夜更的判斷質素、人手和例外個案（兒童、系統、證件失效）不能假設白天那一套自動延長。判斷：24 小時是服務時間，不是降低抽查。

輸入勞工。利：短缺工種有合法渠道，本地工人不必和無許可工作競爭。弊：合約不真、崗位被調去條件不允許的工作、外傭被轉到別的僱主。判斷：輸入是逗留條件，不是開工之後再補手續。

人工智能與執法私隱。利：證件真偽和合資格分流可以更快。弊：收集超過核實身份所需、無法解釋的自動拒絕、沒有人工覆核。判斷：DPP 只收必要資料；機器分流，人決定例外。見保障資料。

24-hour clearance (Huanggang passengers and passenger vehicles). Benefit: fewer people rushing a closing time. Cost: night-shift judgement, staffing, and exceptions (a child, a system failure, an invalid document) do not extend themselves from the day shift. Judgement: 24 hours is the hours of service, not a thinner spot check.

Imported labour. Benefit: a lawful route for trades in shortage, so local workers are not competing with unauthorised work. Cost: a contract that is not genuine, a post moved outside the condition, a domestic helper moved to another employer. Judgement: admission is a condition of stay, not permission to start work and file the papers later.

AI and privacy in enforcement. Benefit: document checks and streaming of eligible people can be faster. Cost: collecting more than identity needs, an automatic refusal nobody can explain, no human review. Judgement: the data principles collect only what is necessary. The machine streams. A person decides the exception. See data protection.

高分發言大綱（約 60–90 秒）Outline (about 60–90 seconds)
  1. 原則：人身安全優先 → 控制場面 → 依法處理 → 事後檢討。
  2. 即時：保持距離與冷靜；呼叫支援；保護同事與其他旅客；必要時啟動安全程序／報警（按部門指引）。
  3. 溝通：簡短說明程序、避免激化；記錄時間、言行、證人。
  4. 跟進：照顧受影響職員；向上級匯報；檢視人手與培訓。
  5. 收束：紀律部隊須法理情兼顧，但不能因暴力而放棄程序公義。
  1. Principle: safety of the person first, then control the scene, then act according to law, then review.
  2. At once: keep distance and stay calm; call for support; protect colleagues and other travellers; start the safety procedure or call the police if needed, following departmental guidelines.
  3. Communication: a short explanation of the procedure, without escalating; record the time, what was said and done, and witnesses.
  4. Follow-up: look after staff who were affected; report to a senior; review staffing and training.
  5. Close: a disciplined service holds law, reason and humanity together, and does not drop procedural fairness because of violence.

Benefits: plug skill gaps; boost innovation; fiscal contribution.

Define efficiency as not the same as being careless. 是否應全面擴大 e-道？Should e-Channel be expanded to everyone?

發言大綱（約 60–90 秒）Outline (about 60–90 seconds)
  1. 立場：擴大給合資格的人，不擴大到「所有人都不經抽查」。
  2. 好處：縮短合資格居民和旅客的隊伍，讓櫃檯處理例外。年報撮要裡的年齡下調、容易飛，只作「已在做」的例子，見口岸主線。
  3. 風險：兒童、證件失效、系統未能識別、尾隨和冒用容貌，都不能靠機器自己收尾。
  4. 做法：清楚的登記資格、人工複核通道、私隱上只收集核實身份所需的資料。
  5. 收束：效率來自分流，保安來自分流之後仍有人負責。
  1. Stance: expand it for eligible people. Do not expand it to “nobody is spot-checked”.
  2. Benefit: shorter queues for eligible residents and visitors, so counters handle exceptions. The lower age and Easy Fly in the annual-report digest are only examples of what is already being done. See the port line.
  3. Risk: a child, an invalid document, a system that cannot identify the person, tailgating, or a misused face cannot be closed by the machine alone.
  4. Method: clear eligibility to register, a channel for a human re-check, and privacy limited to what identity verification needs.
  5. Close: efficiency comes from streaming. Security comes from a person still being responsible after the stream.

打擊假結婚應更側重情報，還是口岸審查？Should bogus-marriage work lean on intelligence, or on checks at the port?

發言大綱（約 60–90 秒）Outline (about 60–90 seconds)
  1. 立場：兩層都要，情報打集團，口岸和登記處處理眼前這一份申請。
  2. 情報：跨部門和跨境合作能看到單個櫃檯看不到的重複模式。易記卡是閃刺 2025，見時事第一則。
  3. 口岸／登記處：文件和關係仍要在當下核對，否則情報名單沒有落地。
  4. 風險：只靠口岸會漏掉組織者；只靠情報會誤把真正夫婦當成名單上的人。
  5. 標準：任何做法都不能降低「有記錄、可請示、不羞辱」這三項。
  6. 收束：接角度 S2，不把假結婚說成家事。
  1. Stance: both layers. Intelligence against the syndicate; the port and the registry deal with the application in front of them.
  2. Intelligence: cross-department and cross-boundary work can see a repeated pattern one counter cannot. The memory card is Flash Spear 2025. See news card 1.
  3. Port / registry: the document and the relationship still have to be checked now, or the intelligence list never lands.
  4. Risk: the port alone misses the organiser. Intelligence alone can treat a genuine couple as a name on a list.
  5. Standard: no method may drop the three items “recorded, able to seek advice, not humiliating”.
  6. Close: join angle S2. Do not call a bogus marriage a family matter.

人才計劃應收緊還是維持開放？Should talent schemes be tightened, or stay open?

發言大綱（約 60–90 秒）Outline (about 60–90 seconds)
  1. 立場：維持有條件的開放。收緊的是真確性。大門仍然開着。
  2. 開放的理由：短缺工種和競爭力。不要臨時發明配額，計劃分別看證件頁。
  3. 風險：假學歷、假受僱、房屋和公共服務壓力。假學歷只用「首次破獲」這個已有例子。
  4. 把關：文件核對、逗留條件、續簽再審、違規之後的執法。
  5. 便利：提早三個月申請延長，改的是遞表時間。條件仍然要核對。見時事第五則。
  6. 收束：質素先於數量；入境處的角色是依法便利和守住入境制度。
  1. Stance: keep a conditional openness. What tightens is genuineness. The door stays open.
  2. Why stay open: trades in shortage, and competitiveness. Do not invent a quota on the spot. Scheme differences are on the identity page.
  3. Risk: fake qualifications, false employment, and pressure on housing and public services. For fake qualifications use only the existing “first detection” example.
  4. Gatekeeping: document checks, conditions of stay, a fresh look at renewal, and enforcement after a breach.
  5. Facilitation: applying three months early changes when the form is filed. The conditions still apply. See news card 5.
  6. Close: quality before quantity. ImmD’s role is lawful facilitation and keeping the entry system intact.

討論可用 角度卡 定調；涉及突發場面可簡用 GSMEAC（先 Ground／Situation／Mission，再講做法）。評分自查：立場清晰、連結 ImmD、聽取他人、建共識、守時、專業用語。Set the tone with an angle card. For a sudden scene, a short GSMEAC is enough (Ground, Situation and Mission first, then what you would do). Self-check: a clear stance, a link to ImmD, listening, building agreement, time, and professional wording.
