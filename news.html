新皇崗口岸正式開通The new Huanggang Port has opened

2026-10-12 政府新聞公報Government announcement 應考卡在時事頁；1-2-3 在演講示例。施政報告原句仍在下面政策撮要。The study card is on the current-affairs page. The 1-2-3 example is on the speech page. The Policy Address sentence stays in the policy digest below.

開通（2026-10-09 聯合公布）。保安局與深圳市人民政府口岸辦公室公布：皇崗口岸港方口岸區及相關延伸區自 2026 年 7 月 31 日零時起啟用，依照香港特別行政區法律實施管轄。新聯檢大樓已驗收，系統測試、試運行及應急演練已完成。口岸於 2026 年 10 月 12 日上午 6 時 30 分正式開通，為旅客和客運車輛提供 24 小時通關，同時暫停往來深圳皇崗口岸臨時旅檢樓和香港落馬洲管制站的旅檢服務。原文：P2026100900280。這份公布沒有寫設計人次，也沒有重寫「一地兩檢」。

模式與設計流量（另一份公布＋施政報告）。2026 年 7 月 17 日，政府歡迎立法會通過《皇崗口岸港方口岸區條例草案》的公布寫明：重建後口岸採用「一地兩檢」及「合作查驗、一次放行」；設計通關流量每日約 20 萬人次，港鐵北環線支線開通後可至每日約 30 萬人次。原文：P2026071700639。施政報告的對應句仍是「預計今年第四季投入服務」，那是計劃；10 月 12 日的開通是該計劃的落實。20 萬和 30 萬是設計流量，不是開通當日實測人次。

設計通關時間（宣布的改善）。行政長官 2026 年 10 月 11 日開通儀式致辭：車輛通關時間由原來約 10 分鐘縮短到少於 2 分鐘；旅客整體通關時間由原來約 30 分鐘縮短到約 5 分鐘。這是為新模式宣布的設計改善，不是入境處單獨公布的開通當日實測。旅客約 30 分鐘至約 5 分鐘，亦見 7 月 17 日公布所寫的「日後」整體通關時間。致辭：政府新聞網。

入境處角度。港方口岸區適用香港法律。身份、逗留條件和例外仍由人員判斷。兩邊查完，才一次放行。兩套法律仍然分開。暫停旅檢的管制站是落馬洲，要和羅湖分開。設計時間說明預期流程。當日並非每一個人都在該分鐘數內放行。

約 20 秒

施政報告說皇崗第四季會一地兩檢、一次放行。公報寫明 10 月 12 日早上 6 時 30 分已經開通，旅客和客運車輛 24 小時通關，落馬洲與臨時旅檢樓的旅檢同時暫停。每日約 20 萬人次是 7 月的設計流量。兩邊查完，才一次放行。

複習索引：JRE public pulse（p66）。數字以政府新聞公報、開通儀式致辭及施政報告原文為準。

運輸署，不是入境處。2026 年 10 月 16 日運輸署就重陽節高峰公布皇崗交通安排：繁忙時段專營巴士可加密至每 2 至 3 分鐘一班，專線小巴及跨境巴士可加密至每 3 至 4 分鐘一班；「香港出行易」新增「皇崗口岸」按鈕。班次不是入境處的表現指標。原文：P2026101600323。完整應考句在時事頁。

The opening (joint announcement of 9 October 2026). The Security Bureau and the Shenzhen Municipal People’s Government Port Office announced that the Hong Kong port area at Huanggang and the related extended areas took effect at 00:00 on 31 July 2026 and are administered under the law of the HKSAR. The new joint-inspection building had been accepted, and system tests, trial runs and emergency drills were complete. The port opened at 6:30 a.m. on 12 October 2026, with 24-hour clearance for passengers and passenger vehicles. Passenger clearance between the temporary passenger building at Shenzhen Huanggang Port and Hong Kong’s Lok Ma Chau Control Point was suspended at the same time. Text: P2026100900280. That announcement does not state a design throughput, and it does not restate “co-location”.

The mode and the design flow (a different announcement, plus the Policy Address). On 17 July 2026 the Government welcomed passage of the Huanggang Port Hong Kong Port Area Bill and stated that the rebuilt port would use co-location and “joint inspection, one release”. The design flow is about 200,000 passenger trips a day, and about 300,000 a day after the MTR Northern Link spur line opens. Text: P2026071700639. The matching Policy Address sentence still says the port was “expected to open in the fourth quarter of this year”. That is the plan. The opening on 12 October carries the plan out. The 200,000 and 300,000 are design flow, not the measured throughput on opening day.

Design clearance time (an announced improvement). In the opening-ceremony speech on 11 October 2026 the Chief Executive said vehicle clearance time would be shortened from about 10 minutes to under 2 minutes, and overall passenger clearance time from about 30 minutes to about 5 minutes. That is the design improvement announced for the new mode, not a measured result published by ImmD alone for the opening day. The passenger half, about 30 minutes to about 5, is also the future overall clearance time in the 17 July announcement. Speech: news.gov.hk.

ImmD angle. The Hong Kong port area is under Hong Kong law. Identity, conditions of stay and exceptions stay with an officer. Both sides finish the check, then one release. The two legal systems stay separate. The suspended passenger clearance is Lok Ma Chau. Keep it apart from Lo Wu. The design times describe the expected process. They do not mean every person that day was released inside those minutes.

About 20 seconds

The Policy Address said Huanggang would open in the fourth quarter with co-location and one release. The announcement states that it opened at 6:30 a.m. on 12 October, with 24-hour clearance for passengers and passenger vehicles, and that passenger clearance between Lok Ma Chau and the temporary passenger building stopped at the same time. About 200,000 trips a day is the July design flow. Both sides finish the check, then one release.

Revision index: JRE public pulse (p66). Figures follow the government announcements, the opening-ceremony speech and the Policy Address text.

Transport Department, not ImmD. On 16 October 2026 the Transport Department announced Huanggang traffic arrangements for the Chung Yeung peak: in busy periods franchised buses may run every 2 to 3 minutes, and green minibuses and cross-boundary coaches every 3 to 4 minutes. HKeMobility adds a “Huanggang Port” button. Those headways are not an ImmD performance figure. Text: P2026101600323. The exam line is on the current-affairs page.

「粵艇南下」正式實施Southbound travel for Guangdong yachts is in force

2026-10-16 政府新聞公報Government announcement 應考卡在時事頁；1-2-3 在演講示例。這不是入境處新聞公報，也不是皇崗那種陸地管制站。The study card is on the current-affairs page. The 1-2-3 example is on the speech page. This is not an ImmD press release, and it is not a land control point like Huanggang.

正式實施（2026-10-16 政府新聞公報）。「粵艇南下」首航儀式於 2026 年 10 月 16 日舉行，標誌計劃正式實施。署理財政司司長、運輸及物流局局長和海事處處長擔任主禮嘉賓，歡迎五艘廣東籍訪港遊艇。五艘是儀式當日迎接的船，不是入境處的人次，也不是配額。計劃接在今年六月「港艇北上」，以及九月粵港簽署的工作安排之後；該工作安排為遊艇跨境互訪建立制度化基礎。有意南下的船東或船長可線上預辦手續，並須在六個指定內地口岸完成離境查驗：廣州南沙客運港口岸、深圳蛇口郵輪母港口岸、深圳機場碼頭口岸、珠海九洲港口岸、珠海萬山港口岸、中山港口岸盛世遊艇會碼頭。這六個是內地口岸，不是香港入境處的管制站。航行範圍達到離岸 20 海里、抗浪能力不少於兩米的廣東籍遊艇可申請訪港。英文公報把同一項船的條件寫成 “up to 20 nautical miles”，不要說成兩個標準。原文：P2026101600508。英文公報：P2026101600631。

入境處角度。牽頭的是運輸及物流局和海事處。入境處守的是船上的人：身份、逗留條件、船員和乘客的出入境檢查。公報附件寫明，抵港前最遲 24 小時經海事處電子業務系統（eBS）提交入境船員及乘客名單。名單是預先申報，供相關部門預先審核。准許入境要等人受檢。人員出入境有兩條路：按確認電郵到指定認可碇泊處接受檢查；或在入港後 24 小時內及離港前 24 小時內，由船長或代表帶同船上所有人士的旅行證件，到入境事務處港口管制組（中環民輝街 32 號中區政府碼頭 2 樓）。附件：流程 PDF。海事處的指定錨泊區是船停哪裡；入境檢查用的認可碇泊處是人在哪裡受檢。不要說成同一個地方。

約 20 秒

遊艇可以南下，但人仍要過入境檢查。10 月 16 日的首航儀式標誌正式實施。五艘是當日迎接的廣東籍遊艇，不是入境處人次。我只記一個入境處事實：抵港前最遲 24 小時經 eBS 提交船員和乘客名單。名單交了，人仍要受檢。

海事處，不是入境處。海事處六月推出的三項便利同樣適用於海外遊艇：升級 eBS，一站式線上辦理入境及關務手續；設立指定錨泊區，並放寬來港前須先在遊艇會預訂泊位的要求；授權內地機構在來港前為船長提供考試及培訓。船東或船長最早可於到港前 30 日內經 eBS 申請「允許在香港水域內航行」，並預約錨泊位置。那是航行允許，不是准許入境。海事處已授權兩間內地考試中心、批核七間內地培訓機構；近 300 人已取得香港水域本地知識資格。這是海事處的資格，不是入境處的通關人次。署理財政司司長致辭裡，超級遊艇產業市場價值預計 2032 年約 3,500 億港元，以及機場、南丫島、香港仔和紅磡四個新遊艇會合共超過 1,100 個泊位，是遊艇經濟的話，不要說成入境處的表現。船長須知另寫：訪港遊艇須辦妥入境處關務；到港證是海事處文件，申領時須附經入境處蓋印的船員名單（表格 ID 207A）。蓋印說明名單經過入境處，不代表海事處代替入境決定。英文程序指引把三個時限分開：衞生署自由無疫通行證最遲抵港前 30 小時；海事處到達前知會最遲 24 小時；入境處船員及乘客名單也是最遲 24 小時。30 小時不是入境處的時限。離開內地水域前 4 小時經內地單一窗口，是內地手續。須知：海事處。指引：英文流程。完整應考句在時事頁。

Official start (government announcement of 16 October 2026). The maiden-voyage ceremony on 16 October 2026 marked the official implementation of southbound travel for Guangdong yachts. The Acting Financial Secretary, the Secretary for Transport and Logistics and the Director of Marine officiated, and welcomed five Guangdong-registered visiting yachts. Five is the number of yachts welcomed that day. It is not an ImmD passenger count, and it is not a quota. The scheme follows northbound travel for Hong Kong yachts, launched in June, and the Guangdong–Hong Kong work arrangements signed in September, which set an institutional basis for cross-boundary yacht visits. Owners and masters may complete procedures online in advance, and must finish Mainland departure inspection at one of six designated ports: the Nansha Ferry Terminal in Guangzhou, Shekou Cruise Homeport in Shenzhen, Shenzhen Airport Ferry Terminal, Jiuzhou Port Passenger Terminal in Zhuhai, Wanshan Port in Zhuhai, and Sunsea Yacht Club Marina at Zhongshan Port. Those six are Mainland ports, not Hong Kong ImmD control points. The English text of the same announcement says a Guangdong-registered yacht may apply if it meets a navigation range of up to 20 nautical miles offshore and a wave-resistance capability of not less than 2 metres. That is the same vessel condition as the Chinese wording. It is not a second standard. Text: P2026101600508. English text: P2026101600631.

ImmD angle. Transport and Logistics and the Marine Department lead the scheme. ImmD is the gate for the people on board: identity, conditions of stay, and clearance of crew and passengers. The attachment says the arrival crew and passenger list must be submitted through the Marine Department’s Electronic Business System (eBS) no later than 24 hours before arrival. The list is an advance declaration for departments to pre-check. Permission to land waits until the people are examined. People clear immigration in one of two ways: at the approved anchorage named in the acknowledgement email, or, within 24 hours after arrival and within 24 hours before departure, the master or a representative brings the travel documents of everyone on board to the Harbour Control Section of ImmD (2/F, Central Government Pier, 32 Man Fai Street, Central). Attachment: flow PDF. A Marine Department designated anchorage is where the vessel may lie. An approved anchorage used for immigration examination is where the people are cleared. Do not treat them as the same place.

About 20 seconds

Yachts may travel southbound, but people still clear immigration. The ceremony on 16 October marked the official start. Five is the number of Guangdong yachts welcomed that day, not an ImmD count. I keep only one ImmD fact: the crew and passenger list goes in through eBS no later than 24 hours before arrival. A submitted list still leaves the people to be examined.

Marine Department, not ImmD. The three facilitation measures the Marine Department launched in June also apply to overseas yachts: an upgraded eBS for one-stop online arrival and port formalities; designated anchorages, with the pre-arrival yacht-club berth booking relaxed; and authorisation for Mainland institutions to examine and train masters before they arrive. An owner or master may apply through eBS for “Permission to Navigate in Hong Kong Waters”, and book an anchorage, as early as 30 days before arrival. That is permission to navigate, not permission to land. The Marine Department has authorised two Mainland examination centres and approved seven Mainland training institutions. Nearly 300 people have already obtained the Hong Kong waters local-knowledge qualification. That is a Marine qualification, not an ImmD clearance count. In the Acting Financial Secretary’s speech, a superyacht-industry market value of about HK$350 billion by 2032, and more than 1,100 berths at four new yacht clubs at the airport, Lamma Island, Aberdeen and Hung Hom, are remarks about the yacht economy. Do not describe them as ImmD performance. The notes for visiting-yacht masters add that ImmD port formalities must be completed. The port clearance certificate is a Marine Department document. The application must attach the crew list on form ID 207A stamped by ImmD. The stamp shows that the list has passed ImmD. It does not mean the Marine Department makes the immigration decision. The English procedure guide keeps three deadlines apart: Department of Health free pratique, no later than 30 hours before arrival; the Marine Department’s pre-arrival notification, no later than 24 hours; and ImmD’s crew and passenger list, also no later than 24 hours. Thirty hours is not ImmD’s deadline. The Mainland Single Window, four hours before leaving Mainland waters, is a Mainland step. Notes: Marine Department. Guide: English flow.
