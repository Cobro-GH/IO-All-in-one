新皇崗口岸正式開通The new Huanggang Port has opened

2026-10-12 政府新聞公報Government announcement 應考卡在時事頁；1-2-3 在演講示例。施政報告原句仍在下面政策撮要。The study card is on the current-affairs page. The 1-2-3 example is on the speech page. The Policy Address sentence stays in the policy digest below.

開通（2026-10-09 聯合公布）。保安局與深圳市人民政府口岸辦公室公布：皇崗口岸港方口岸區及相關延伸區自 2026 年 7 月 31 日零時起啟用，依照香港特別行政區法律實施管轄。新聯檢大樓已驗收，系統測試、試運行及應急演練已完成。口岸於 2026 年 10 月 12 日上午 6 時 30 分正式開通，為旅客和客運車輛提供 24 小時通關，同時暫停往來深圳皇崗口岸臨時旅檢樓和香港落馬洲管制站的旅檢服務。原文：P2026100900280。這份公布沒有寫設計人次，也沒有重寫「一地兩檢」。

模式與設計流量（另一份公布＋施政報告）。2026 年 7 月 17 日，政府歡迎立法會通過《皇崗口岸港方口岸區條例草案》的公布寫明：重建後口岸採用「一地兩檢」及「合作查驗、一次放行」；設計通關流量每日約 20 萬人次，港鐵北環線支線開通後可至每日約 30 萬人次。原文：P2026071700639。施政報告的對應句仍是「預計今年第四季投入服務」，那是計劃；10 月 12 日的開通是該計劃的落實。20 萬和 30 萬是設計流量，不是開通當日實測人次。

設計通關時間（不是實測）。行政長官 2026 年 10 月 11 日開通儀式致辭：車輛通關時間由原來約 10 分鐘縮短到少於 2 分鐘；旅客整體通關時間由原來約 30 分鐘縮短到約 5 分鐘。這是為新模式宣布的設計改善，不是入境處單獨公布的開通當日實測。旅客約 30 分鐘至約 5 分鐘，亦見 7 月 17 日公布所寫的「日後」整體通關時間。致辭：政府新聞網。

入境處角度。港方口岸區適用香港法律，身份、逗留條件和例外仍由人員判斷。一次放行是查完才放，不是取消其中一方的檢查。暫停旅檢的管制站是落馬洲，不是羅湖。設計時間說明預期流程，不代表當日每一個人都在該分鐘數內放行。

約 20 秒

施政報告說皇崗第四季會一地兩檢、一次放行。公報寫明 10 月 12 日早上 6 時 30 分已經開通，旅客和客運車輛 24 小時通關，落馬洲與臨時旅檢樓的旅檢同時暫停。每日約 20 萬人次是 7 月的設計流量。一次放行不是不檢查。

運輸署，不是入境處。2026 年 10 月 16 日運輸署就重陽節高峰公布皇崗交通安排：繁忙時段專營巴士可加密至每 2 至 3 分鐘一班，專線小巴及跨境巴士可加密至每 3 至 4 分鐘一班；「香港出行易」新增「皇崗口岸」按鈕。班次不是入境處的表現指標。原文：P2026101600323。完整應考句在時事頁。

The opening (joint announcement of 9 October 2026). The Security Bureau and the Shenzhen Municipal People’s Government Port Office announced that the Hong Kong port area at Huanggang and the related extended areas took effect at 00:00 on 31 July 2026 and are administered under the law of the HKSAR. The new joint-inspection building had been accepted, and system tests, trial runs and emergency drills were complete. The port opened at 6:30 a.m. on 12 October 2026, with 24-hour clearance for passengers and passenger vehicles. Passenger clearance between the temporary passenger building at Shenzhen Huanggang Port and Hong Kong’s Lok Ma Chau Control Point was suspended at the same time. Text: P2026100900280. That announcement does not state a design throughput, and it does not restate “co-location”.

The mode and the design flow (a different announcement, plus the Policy Address). On 17 July 2026 the Government welcomed passage of the Huanggang Port Hong Kong Port Area Bill and stated that the rebuilt port would use co-location and “joint inspection, one release”. The design flow is about 200,000 passenger trips a day, and about 300,000 a day after the MTR Northern Link spur line opens. Text: P2026071700639. The matching Policy Address sentence still says the port was “expected to open in the fourth quarter of this year”. That is the plan. The opening on 12 October carries the plan out. The 200,000 and 300,000 are design flow, not the measured throughput on opening day.

Design clearance time (not a measured result). In the opening-ceremony speech on 11 October 2026 the Chief Executive said vehicle clearance time would be shortened from about 10 minutes to under 2 minutes, and overall passenger clearance time from about 30 minutes to about 5 minutes. That is the design improvement announced for the new mode, not a measured result published by ImmD alone for the opening day. The passenger half, about 30 minutes to about 5, is also the future overall clearance time in the 17 July announcement. Speech: news.gov.hk.

ImmD angle. The Hong Kong port area is under Hong Kong law. Identity, conditions of stay and exceptions are still a person’s judgement. One release means release after the inspection. It does not cancel either side’s check. The control point whose passenger clearance was suspended is Lok Ma Chau, not Lo Wu. The design times describe the expected process. They do not mean every person that day was released inside those minutes.

About 20 seconds

The Policy Address said Huanggang would open in the fourth quarter with co-location and one release. The announcement states that it opened at 6:30 a.m. on 12 October, with 24-hour clearance for passengers and passenger vehicles, and that passenger clearance between Lok Ma Chau and the temporary passenger building stopped at the same time. About 200,000 trips a day is the July design flow. One release is not the absence of a check.

Transport Department, not ImmD. On 16 October 2026 the Transport Department announced Huanggang traffic arrangements for the Chung Yeung peak: in busy periods franchised buses may run every 2 to 3 minutes, and green minibuses and cross-boundary coaches every 3 to 4 minutes.
