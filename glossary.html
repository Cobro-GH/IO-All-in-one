面試公式（Notes_v1）Interview formula (Notes v1)

「入境事務主任必須嚴格但公平地執法，便利合法流動，維護國家安全及公共秩序，並為居民及訪客提供專業服務。」

“An Immigration Officer must enforce the law strictly but fairly, facilitate lawful movement, safeguard national security and public order, and provide a professional service to residents and visitors.”

簽證／進入許可是入境處落實入境政策的主要工具……透過逗留條件管理在港活動，並與違反逗留條件、非法工作及移送離境制度相連。
A visa or entry permit is ImmD’s main tool for carrying out immigration policy. Conditions of stay manage activities in Hong Kong, and connect to breach of conditions, illegal work and removal.
高才通同時做兩件事：服務經濟發展，也核對申請是否真確、身份是否清楚、是否符合準則。
The Top Talent Pass does two jobs: it serves economic development, and it checks that the application is genuine, the identity is clear, and the criteria are met.

高頻模擬題（可展開自練）Frequent practice questions

  1. 你對入境處服務有何認識？答綱在面試 §20.1，六類骨架在服務地圖。What do you know about ImmD’s services? The outline is interview §20.1. The six-part skeleton is the service map.
  2. 如何平衡便利與管制？用§20.2：e-道和網上服務提高效率，身份核實和風險評估仍然有效。可接角度 1＋6。How do you balance facilitation and control? Use §20.2: e-Channel and online services raise efficiency, and identity checks and risk assessment still work. Join Angles 1 and 6.
  3. 哪類服務最能反映主任專業？用§20.3：簽證審批和出入境檢查，因為同時考法例、文件真偽和服務態度。Which service best shows professionalism? Use §20.3: visa assessment and arrival examination, because they test the law, document authenticity and manner together.
  4. 入境處網站如何幫助你理解角色？網站把簽證、證件、登記、管制站和新聞分開列出，剛好對上「執法＋公共服務」。準備時用它核對服務名稱，不要背已經改版的按鈕。How does the ImmD website help you understand the role? It lists visas, documents, registration, control points and news separately, which matches “enforcement plus public service”. Use it to check service names. Do not memorise a button that has already been redesigned.
  5. 哪些法例與 IO 最相關？先講 Cap.115 和 Cap.331 的分工，再提身份與護照。口頭版在面試頁，條次場景在法例頁。Which laws matter most to an IO? First separate Cap. 115 from Cap. 331, then identity and the passport. The spoken version is on the interview page. Section scenes are on the law page.
  6. 誠信對 IO 意味著甚麼？無人看見也按同一程序；有人請你通融就拒絕、記錄、上報。填空骨架在誠信 STAR。What does integrity mean for an IO? The same procedure when nobody is watching. If someone asks for a favour, refuse, record and report. The fill-in skeleton is the integrity STAR.
  7. 當前入境問題你應了解哪些？五則新聞和六條大灣區主線在時事頁。每題只取一則，加上對應角度，不要五則連讀。Which current immigration issues should you know? The five news cards and six Greater Bay Area lines are on the current-affairs page. Take one card per question, plus the matching angle. Do not read all five in a row.
