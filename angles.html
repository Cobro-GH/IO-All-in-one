Part 1　八大角度Part 1 — eight angles

角度 1　安全與便利 Angle 1　Security vs Facilitation

適用：科技、e-道、智慧口岸、簽證、大灣區流動Use for: technology, e-Channel, smart ports, visas, Greater Bay Area movement

便利合法流動至關重要，但絕不能犧牲邊境安全。 While facilitating legitimate movement is essential, it must never compromise border security.

中文

負責任的入境制度必須在安全與便利之間取得審慎平衡。e-道及生物辨識等工具同時提升效率與準確性。有人認為嚴格安檢只會造成長龍。然而，科技已證明兩者相輔相成。對入境事務主任而言，維持這個平衡需要堅定與良好判斷力。

English

A responsible immigration system must strike a careful balance between security and facilitation. Modern tools such as e-Channel and biometric verification improve both efficiency and accuracy. Some argue that strict security only creates long queues. However, technology has proven the two goals are complementary. For an Immigration Officer, maintaining this balance requires both firmness and good judgement.

角度 2　國家安全與一國兩制 Angle 2　National Security & One Country Two Systems

適用：國安、假結婚、有組織犯罪Use for: national security, bogus marriage, organised crime

維護國家安全是『一國兩制』成功實踐的根本前提。 Safeguarding national security is the fundamental premise of ‘One Country, Two Systems’.

中文

國家安全透過管制站的日常工作具體保障。假結婚等欺詐行為可能為不受歡迎人士打開通道。有人認為談國安是過度政治化。事實上，安全是其他一切的前提。入境事務主任在履行職責時必須把國家安全放在心上。

English

National security is protected through concrete daily work at control points. Immigration fraud such as fake marriages can open pathways for undesirable elements. Some claim talking about national security is over-politicising. In reality, security is the prerequisite for everything else. An Immigration Officer must always keep national security in mind.

角度 3　人才引進與經濟活力 Angle 3　Talent Admission & Economic Vitality

適用：人才計劃、高才通、競爭力Use for: talent schemes, Top Talent Pass, competitiveness

香港的長遠繁榮取決於吸引合適人才，同時防止濫用。 Hong Kong’s prosperity depends on attracting the right talent while preventing abuse.

中文

人才是競爭力的關鍵。各項計劃已吸引逾23萬人來港，2026年3月起延長申請可提早三個月提交。然而，首次破獲偽造學歷申請高才通的案件顯示，開放必須配合把關。入境事務主任發揮守門人角色，確保計劃服務香港長遠利益。

English

Talent is a key driver of competitiveness. Over 230,000 people have been admitted under various schemes, and from March 2026 extension applications can be submitted three months early. However, the first case of fake academic credentials for the Top Talent Pass Scheme shows that openness must be matched by safeguards. Immigration Officers play a gatekeeping role to protect Hong Kong’s long-term interests.

角度 4　打擊非法勞工 Angle 4　Combating Illegal Employment

適用：非法勞工、本地就業優先Use for: illegal employment, local workers’ priority

非法勞工損害本地工人優先權及入境管制完整性。堅決而專業的執法必要。 Illegal employment undermines local workers’ priority and immigration integrity. Firm yet professional enforcement is necessary.

中文

保障本地工人就業權利是政府明確優先事項。僱主最高刑罰已提高至罰款50萬元及監禁10年，2025年更成立由保安局局長擔任召集人的跨部門專責組。有人稱部分行業缺乏本地工人。正確做法是透過合法渠道及改善條件，而非容忍違法。入境事務主任必須堅定執法，同時保持專業及不帶歧視。

English

Protecting local workers’ employment rights is a clear government priority. The maximum penalty for employers has been raised to a fine of $500,000 and 10 years’ imprisonment, and a high-level Interdepartmental Task Force led by the Secretary for Security was established in 2025. Some claim certain sectors lack local workers. The proper solution is legal channels and better conditions, not tolerating illegality. An Immigration Officer must enforce the law firmly while remaining professional and non-discriminatory.

角度 5　專業服務與公眾信任 Angle 5　Professional Service & Public Trust

適用：服務質素、EQ、誠信、公眾形象Use for: service quality, EQ, integrity, public image

每一次互動都影響公眾信心。專業精神意味著堅定之餘仍保持禮貌。 Every interaction shapes public confidence. Professionalism means firmness with courtesy.

中文

公眾信任是有效入境管制的基礎。主任如何對待公眾直接影響部門形象。有人擔心過於禮貌會顯得軟弱。事實上，真正的專業是既堅定又有禮。軟弱來自不一致，而非尊重。溝通及情緒智商的持續培訓至關重要。

English

Public trust is the foundation of effective immigration control. How officers treat the public directly affects the Department’s image. Some worry that being too polite makes officers look weak. In fact, true professionalism is the ability to be both firm and courteous. Weakness comes from inconsistency, not from respect. Continuous training in communication and emotional intelligence is essential.

角度 6　科技創新與把關 Angle 6　Technology & Innovation with Safeguards

適用：AI、生物辨識、e-道、效率Use for: AI, biometrics, e-Channel, efficiency

科技是有力工具，但必須配合人員的專業判斷。 Technology is a powerful enabler, but it must be paired with human professional judgement.

中文

科技已改變入境管制。e-道及生物辨識同時提升速度與準確性。然而，科技永遠無法完全取代受訓人員的判斷。有人擔心過度依賴會令主任懶散。相反，科技讓主任專注需要同理心與判斷的高風險個案。最好的制度是智能科技與專業主任相結合。

English

Technology has transformed immigration control. e-Channel and biometrics improve speed and accuracy. However, technology can never fully replace a trained officer’s judgement. Some worry over-reliance will make officers lazy. On the contrary, technology frees officers to focus on high-risk cases that require empathy and judgement. The best system combines smart technology with well-trained officers.

角度 7　大灣區與區域聯繫 Angle 7　Greater Bay Area & Regional Connectivity

適用：北部都會區、跨境流動、大灣區Use for: Northern Metropolis, cross-boundary flow, Greater Bay Area

入境事務主任在促進無縫而又安全的跨境流動中扮演關鍵角色。 Immigration Officers play a pivotal role in facilitating seamless yet secure cross-boundary movement.

中文

大灣區連接是重大戰略機遇。跨境流動增加，需要相應加強入境管制能力。有人擔心更緊密融合會令香港失去獨特優勢。在『一國兩制』下，香港優勢得到保留，更好連接反而令優勢倍增。入境事務主任把無縫而又安全流動的願景轉化為現實。

English

Greater Bay Area connectivity is a major strategic opportunity. Increased cross-boundary flow requires corresponding strengthening of immigration capacity. Some fear closer integration will make Hong Kong lose its unique advantages. Under ‘One Country, Two Systems’, Hong Kong’s strengths are preserved and better connectivity multiplies them. Immigration Officers turn the vision of seamless yet secure movement into reality.

角度 8　入境事務主任的個人質素 Angle 8　Personal Qualities of an Immigration Officer

適用：幾乎所有題目（尤其次要角度或結論）Use for: almost every question, especially a secondary angle or the close

政策與科技的成效取決於執行人員。 Policies and technology are only as effective as the officers who implement them.

中文

入境管制的質素取決於主任的質素。誠信、情緒智商及壓力下保持冷靜不可或缺。有人認為軟技能不及嚴格執法重要。事實上，最好的主任既能堅定執法，又能以尊嚴對待他人。持續自我提升是終身責任。

English

The quality of immigration control depends on the quality of its officers. Integrity, emotional intelligence and calmness under pressure are indispensable. Some think soft skills are secondary to strict enforcement. In reality, the best officers enforce the law firmly while treating people with dignity. Continuous self-improvement is a lifelong responsibility.

S1　非法勞工 S1　Illegal employment

易記鉤：僱主最高罰款 50 萬元及監禁 10 年；2025 年保安局局長跨部門專責組Hook: employer fine up to $500,000 and 10 years; 2025 task force chaired by the Secretary for Security

打擊非法勞工必須堅決，同時專業、不歧視，並同時看僱主責任和工人身份。 Action against illegal employment must be firm, professional and non-discriminatory, and must address both the employer and the worker.

中文

非法受僱破壞的是本地工人優先就業，以及逗留條件這套管制。只處理現場的工人、不處理聘用他們的人，違法就會變成可以計算的成本。角度筆記已寫明僱主最高刑罰提高至罰款 50 萬元及監禁 10 年，並在 2025 年設立由保安局局長擔任召集人的跨部門專責組。有人說個別行業聘不到本地工人。正確的回應是合法輸入和改善條件，而不是容忍無許可工作。主任執法時要堅定，也要避免因國籍或行業而歧視。全文背景見時事第二則。

English

Illegal employment undermines local workers’ priority and the condition-of-stay system. Focusing only on the worker on site, and not on the person who hired them, turns the breach into a cost that can be priced in. The study notes record that the maximum penalty for employers has been raised to a fine of $500,000 and 10 years’ imprisonment, and that a task force chaired by the Secretary for Security was set up in 2025. Some say certain sectors cannot find local workers. The proper response is legal admission and better conditions, not tolerating unauthorised work. An officer enforces the law firmly without discriminating by nationality or trade.

S2　假結婚 S2　Bogus marriage

易記鉤：閃刺 2025，Drive 卡約 60 宗＋750 萬；核對新聞稿Hook: Flash Spear 2025; Drive card about 60 cases and $7.5 million — check the press release

假結婚是入境欺詐。必須遏止濫用，同時避免誤傷真正的婚姻。 A bogus marriage is immigration fraud. Abuse must be stopped without mistreating genuine couples.

中文

沒有真實夫妻關係，卻用婚姻取得本不會批出的逗留，傷害的是登記制度和入境管制。Drive 易記卡把 2025 年「閃刺」收成約 60 宗、約 750 萬元，並寫明聯同廣東瓦解集團；這是備考鉤子，不是可以不核對就在考場背出的最新統計。情報合作處理集團，管制站和登記處處理眼前這份文件和這段關係。嚴查不等於假定每一對跨境外籍組合都是假的。專業的做法是按風險提問、記錄、請示，讓真正的夫婦得到有尊嚴的程序。口述稿見時事第一則。

English

Using a marriage that is not genuine to obtain a stay that would otherwise be refused undermines both the registry and immigration control. The Drive memory card records Operation 閃刺 in 2025 as about 60 cases and about $7.5 million, disrupted jointly with Guangdong. That is a revision hook, not a figure to recite as the latest official total without checking the press release. Intelligence work targets syndicates; the control point and the registry deal with the document and the relationship in front of them. Firm checks do not mean treating every cross-boundary couple as bogus. A professional officer asks according to risk, records the answers, and seeks advice, so that genuine couples still receive a dignified process.

S3　非法入境與逾期逗留 S3　Unlawful entry and overstaying

易記鉤：持續遣返；年報撮要 2025 年逾 2,600 名聲請不獲確立人士，約 +18%Hook: continued removal; annual-report digest, 2025, more than 2,600 people whose claims were not substantiated, about +18%

對非法入境和逾期逗留要堅決而有效地執行離境，同時以尊嚴對待每一個人。 Unlawful entry and overstaying require firm and effective removal, with every person treated with dignity.

中文

人如果沒有許可而入境，或許可到期後仍留下，管制就不能停在口頭警告。執行要快，是因為拖延會削弱限期逗留這項條件的意義。年報撮要寫 2025 年遣離超過 2,600 名免遣返聲請不獲確立的人士，較前一年增加約 18%；這句只說明「聲請完結之後的執行」，不能用來宣稱所有聲請都是濫用。答題時把遣送和遞解分開。情緒激動或有健康需要的人，仍先處理安全和法定程序，再談離境安排。詳見時事第四則。

English

Where a person enters without permission, or remains after permission expires, control cannot stop at a verbal warning. Speed matters because delay empties the time limit of its meaning. The annual-report excerpt records that in 2025 more than 2,600 people whose non-refoulement claims were not substantiated were removed, about 18% more than the year before. That describes enforcement after a claim has failed. It is not a basis for saying every claim is abusive. In an answer, keep removal and deportation apart. If the person is distressed or needs medical care, deal with safety and the lawful procedure first, and the removal arrangements second.

S4　偽造證件與身份欺詐 S4　Forged documents and identity fraud

易記鉤：智能身份證、生物辨識、人員持續學習；不另造破案數字Hook: smart identity card, biometrics, officers keep learning — no invented detection figure

身份制度是入境管制的核心。科技幫助辨偽，但不能取代受訓人員的判斷。 The identity system is the core of immigration control. Technology helps detect fraud, but it cannot replace a trained officer’s judgement.

中文

護照、簽證頁、聘書和學歷一旦可以偽造，後面的逗留條件和人才計劃都會被冒用。智能身份證、晶片和生物辨識提高核對速度，也提高發現變造的機會。系統未能讀取、人貌與證件不符、或文件內容互相矛盾時，仍要由人員決定是進一步查問、拒絕入境還是轉介。把責任完全交給機器，會讓主任在例外個案裡沒有角色。這條可與角度 6 並用；假學歷的政策例子在時事第三則，法例場景見 s.42。

English

Once a passport, visa page, contract or academic record can be forged, every later condition of stay and talent scheme can be misused. Smart identity cards, chips and biometrics speed up checking and make alteration easier to see. When the system cannot read the document, the face does not match, or the papers contradict each other, an officer still decides whether to enquire further, refuse permission or refer the case. Handing the whole task to a machine leaves no one responsible for the exception. This angle pairs with Angle 6. The fake-credential example sits with the talent news card, and the citation pattern for false statements sits with section 42 on the law page.

S5　免遣返聲請與困難個案 S5　Non-refoulement and hard cases

易記鉤：公平與效率；延誤同時傷害真正聲請人和公信Hook: fairness and speed; delay harms both a genuine claimant and public confidence

免遣返聲請必須同時做到公平和有效，並且防止機制被濫用。 Non-refoulement claims must be both fair and effective, and the process must be protected from abuse.

中文

聲請制度存在，是因為有些人不能被送回面對危害的地方。程序如果慢到沒有盡頭，真正有需要的人等不到決定，公眾也會認為機制只是拖延離境的方法。防止濫用的方法是盡快而公正地篩選、在聲請不獲確立後執行離境，而不是在未審前就假設每一個聲請人都在說謊。年報裡的遣離數字描述的是不獲確立之後的執行，見 S3，不要把它當成「聲請成功率」的替代指標。主任在困難個案裡的角色，是按權限處理身份、羈留和轉介，並如實記錄，而不是在櫃檯上自行改寫審核標準。

English

The claim process exists because some people cannot be returned to a place where they face harm. If the process has no end, people with a genuine need never receive a decision, and the public comes to see the system as a way to postpone departure. The way to limit abuse is to screen quickly and fairly, and to carry out removal after a claim is not substantiated — not to assume, before any screening, that every claimant is lying. Removal figures in the annual report describe what happens after a claim fails. They are not a substitute for a success rate. An officer’s role in a hard case is to deal with identity, detention and referral within his or her authority, and to record the facts, not to rewrite the screening standard at the counter.

Part 5　標準結尾公式Part 5 — standard closing formula

中文

「總括而言，[重申立場]。作為有志成為入境事務主任的申請人，我承諾嚴格而公平地執法，便利合法旅客及人才流動，維護國家安全及公共秩序，並為居民及訪客提供專業服務。只有堅守這些原則，香港才能在『一國兩制』下繼續作為一個既安全又開放的國際城市蓬勃發展。」

English

“In conclusion, [restate stance]. As an aspiring Immigration Officer, I am committed to applying the law strictly but fairly, facilitating legitimate travellers and talent, safeguarding national security and public order, and delivering professional services. Only by upholding these principles can Hong Kong continue to thrive as a secure yet open international city under ‘One Country, Two Systems’.”

口號Slogan 嚴格而公平　便利真正人士　守護邊界　專業服務 Strictly but fairly · Facilitate legitimate travellers · Safeguard the boundary · Professional service
