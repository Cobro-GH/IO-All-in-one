形式Format

  • 官方：最後面試為甄選一環（2026 周期暫定 2027 年 2–4 月）。
  • 公開分享：約 3 考官×1 考生；約 30–45 分鐘；粵／英／普可能交替；自我介紹、個人、情景、部門／時事、追問。
  • Official: the final interview is one stage of selection (the 2026 cycle is tentatively February–April 2027).
  • Public accounts: about three interviewers and one candidate; about 30–45 minutes; Cantonese, English and Putonghua may alternate; self-introduction, personal questions, a scenario, the department or current affairs, and follow-ups.

四大高頻答法（Drive Note V3 §20 改寫成網頁章節）Four frequent answers (Note V3 §20, rewritten as page sections)

20.1 「你對入境處服務有何認識？」20.1 “What do you know about ImmD’s services?”

答綱Outline

入境處不止查護照：① 簽證按目的處理人才／外傭／學生／旅客等，並用逗留條件管理活動；② 旅行證件（護照、回港證、DI、海員證）保障可信旅行文件；③ 身份證／智能證支援身份、居留權、通關便利；④ 管制站審查＋e-道／非觸式／境外自助提升效率；⑤ 民事登記、外遊協助、電子服務顯示「執法＋公共服務」雙重角色。

ImmD does more than check passports: (1) visas handle talent, domestic helpers, students and visitors according to purpose, and conditions of stay manage activities; (2) travel documents (passport, Re-entry Permit, DI, seafarer’s identity book) keep travel documents trustworthy; (3) the identity card / smart card supports identity, right of abode and easier clearance; (4) examination at control points, plus e-Channel, contactless channels and enrolment outside Hong Kong, raises efficiency; (5) civil registration, assistance abroad and electronic services show the dual role of enforcement and public service.

20.2 「如何平衡便利與管制？」20.2 “How do you balance facilitation and control?”

答綱Outline

便利與管制非矛盾。e-道、網上簽證、證件服務站提高效率，前提是身份核實、防偽、逗留條件、風險評估仍有效。對合資格人士提供便利；對可疑個案依法進一步審查。可接角度 1＋6（見角度表）。

Facilitation and control are not opposites. e-Channel, online visas and document kiosks raise efficiency, on the condition that identity checks, anti-forgery, conditions of stay and risk assessment still work. Eligible people are facilitated. A doubtful case is examined further according to law. Join Angles 1 and 6 (see the angle page).

20.3 「哪類服務最能反映主任專業？」20.3 “Which service best shows an officer’s professionalism?”

答綱Outline

簽證審批與出入境檢查：要準確理解法例政策、判斷目的真偽、辨識文件風險，同時保持服務態度。身份證／旅行證件工作同樣守護身份制度可信性。

Visa assessment and arrival examination: you must understand the law and policy accurately, judge whether the purpose is genuine, spot document risk, and still keep a service manner. Identity-card and travel-document work likewise protects trust in the identity system.

「哪些法律最相關？」“Which laws matter most?”

答綱Outline

首推 Cap.115（管制、逗留條件、非法就業、遣送等）與 Cap.331（紀律部隊權力與操守）；再提 177／539 等身份與護照制度。細節以現行法例為準，主任須懂「賦權邏輯」而非背罰則表。

Start with Cap. 115 (control, conditions of stay, illegal employment, removal) and Cap. 331 (powers and conduct of a disciplined service). Then mention 177 / 539 and the identity and passport system. Detail follows the current ordinance. An officer must understand the logic of the power, not recite a penalty table.

  4. E：帶離嘈雜區、進一步查問、保全證據、請示／轉介、必要時拒絕入境或拘捕（按權限）
  5. A：翻譯、人手、紀錄、醫療
  6. C：上級匯報、跨部門、事後檢討
  1. G: which counter or second-line area, the flow beside it, and whether a vulnerable traveller is there
  2. S: what looks wrong on the document, the traveller’s state, and whether there is an immediate safety risk
  3. M: verify identity according to law, keep order, do not discriminate
  4. E: move away from the noisy area, enquire further, secure evidence, seek advice or refer, and if needed refuse entry or arrest within authority
  5. A: interpretation, staff, a record, medical care
  6. C: report to a senior, other departments, and a review afterwards

GSMEAC 部署分流／關愛通道／廣播＋人手；同時帶政策視野：大灣區／大型賽事顯示跨境合作與智慧通關趨勢——便利≠放鬆把關。

Use GSMEAC to deploy streaming, a care channel, announcements and staff. Add the policy view: the Greater Bay Area and major events show cross-boundary cooperation and smarter clearance. Facilitation is not a looser check.

個人／誠信／下屬管理Personal questions, integrity and supervising staff

練習骨架Practice skeleton 下面是 STAR 空格，不是某人的真實履歷。把自己的經歷填進同一結構；語言轉換時保持 S→T→A→R，不要臨時換故事。The STAR blanks below are not someone’s real résumé. Put your own experience into the same structure. When you change language, keep S → T → A → R. Do not swap the story on the spot.

動機：為什麼是入境處，不是海關Motive: why ImmD, not Customs

  1. S：一次你要同時對人和對規則負責的經歷（輪班、活動、小組作業都可以）。
  2. T：你的任務是既完成手續，又不讓隊裡的人失控。
  3. A：你如何核對事實、向當事人解釋下一步、並接受督導。
  4. R：結果是事情依程序做完。然後點題：入境處管的是人、身份和逗留條件；海關管的是貨物和關稅。你接受輪班、訓練營和紀律，因為管制站不是辦公室時間。
  1. S: one occasion when you were responsible both for people and for a rule (a shift, an event or a group assignment is enough).
  2. T: your task was to finish the procedure and not let the team lose control.
  3. A: how you checked the facts, explained the next step to the person, and accepted supervision.
  4. R: the matter was finished according to the procedure. Then the point: ImmD deals with people, identity and conditions of stay; Customs deals with goods and duty. You accept shifts, residential training and discipline, because a control point is not office hours.

誠信：有人請你通融Integrity: someone asks you to bend the rule

  1. S：同事、朋友或旅客請你跳過一個步驟，例如不記錄、先放行、或在申請上幫一句不實的話。
  2. T：你要守程序，同時不在公眾場合羞辱對方。
  3. A：當面拒絕這項請求，說明你沒有這項酌情權；把對話和文件照實記下；按機制告訴上級，而不是只在事後向朋友抱怨。
  4. R：關係可能變差，但程序沒有被打開缺口。收句：無人看見時仍按同一標準，才是誠信。
  1. S: a colleague, a friend or a traveller asks you to skip a step — not to record it, to let someone through first, or to add an untrue sentence to an application.
  2. T: you must keep the procedure, and not humiliate the person in public.
  3. A: refuse the request to their face and say you do not have that discretion; record the conversation and the documents as they are; tell a senior through the proper channel, rather than only complaining to a friend afterwards.
  4. R: the relationship may worsen, but the procedure has not been opened. Close: integrity is using the same standard when nobody is watching.

下屬當眾頂撞A subordinate challenges you in public

  1. S：櫃檯或隊裡，下屬在旅客面前反駁你的指示，場面開始亂。
  2. T：先恢復秩序，再處理對錯，不能當眾比賽權威。
  3. A：用一句短指示穩定旅客服務；把討論移到旁邊。私下聽他的理由。如果他指出的是安全或事實錯誤，就改；如果只是不服從，就重申標準。記錄發生了什麼，需要時向上級報告。之後對其他組員用同一標準。
  4. R：旅客沒有看見一場內訌；下屬知道頂撞不會被報復，也不會變成新規矩。
  1. S: at a counter or in the team, a subordinate contradicts your instruction in front of a traveller, and the scene starts to fall apart.
  2. T: restore order first, then deal with who was right. Do not compete for authority in public.
  3. A: one short instruction to steady the service to the traveller; move the discussion aside. Hear the reason in private. If it is a safety or factual error, change. If it is only disobedience, restate the standard. Record what happened and report upward if needed. Afterwards use the same standard with the rest of the team.
  4. R: the traveller did not see an internal quarrel. The subordinate knows that a challenge will not be punished for its own sake, and will not become the new rule.

另外準備 6 至 8 個自己的經歷，覆蓋團隊、壓力、錯誤、服務和利益。每個只記四句，不要記演講稿。Also prepare six to eight experiences of your own, covering a team, pressure, a mistake, service and a conflict of interest. Remember four sentences for each. Do not memorise a speech.
