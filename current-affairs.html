怎樣用。角度 1＋6。私隱用 DPP 的「收集目的、保安、查閱」一句帶過，見法例頁，不要把人面辨識說成沒有法律界限。

What it is. e-Channel, contactless channels and facial recognition take eligible people out of the counter queue. The annual-report digest also says the eligible age for e-Channel was lowered from 11 to 7, and that “Easy Fly e-Channel” at the airport lets eligible Hong Kong residents arrive by facial recognition. The Policy Address digest covers the “Smart Crossing” pilot at the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge (contactless clearance from the vehicle), and co-location with “joint inspection, one release” at the rebuilt Huanggang Port, then expected in the fourth quarter. The opening at 6:30 a.m. on 12 October 2026 is that plan carried out. The design flow and the measured two-hour peaks after the opening are on the new Huanggang Port card. Do not memorise a second passenger total in this paragraph. Southbound travel for Guangdong yachts is another facilitation channel, not a land control point like Huanggang: vessel formalities sit with the Marine Department, and the people on board still clear immigration. See the yacht card. The Shenzhen Bay Halloween arrangement is a two-night special extension, from midnight to 2am, not a second port that is normally open 24 hours. See the Shenzhen Bay card. The report and the Policy Address are at the annual report and the Policy Address.

Why ImmD cares. A new mode changes how the check is done. The check itself stays. A child, an invalid document, a system that cannot identify the person, or someone who is not yet registered still comes back to a human decision.

How to use it. Angles 1 and 6. For privacy, one DPP line is enough: purpose of collection, security, and access. See the law page. Do not describe facial recognition as having no legal limit.

4　創科4　Innovation and technology

是甚麼。河套、新田一類合作區是人才和科研人員跨境活動的背景，不是入境處去營運園區。本站沒有把河套寫成皇崗那種一地兩檢或合作查驗口岸；合作查驗的例子只用皇崗。與主任直接有關的是 TechTAS 這類配額：合資格科企輸入科技人才，人一旦停止該份工作，逗留條件可能不再成立。

入境處為何關心。創科政策會增加簽證、短期培訓和往來頻密的個案。2026 年施政報告有「新增短期培訓簽證」的方向，見新聞頁，細則以實施時的簽證規例為準。

怎樣用。答「科技會否取代主任」時分開兩層：通道上的生物辨識是角度 6；人才計劃裡的創科配額是角度 3。兩層都要落到「人仍要判斷例外」。

What it is. Cooperation zones such as the Lok Ma Chau Loop and San Tin are the setting for cross-boundary movement of talent and researchers. ImmD does not run the parks. This site does not describe the Loop as co-location or joint inspection of the Huanggang kind. Use Huanggang for joint inspection. What concerns an officer directly is a quota such as TechTAS: an eligible technology enterprise brings in technology talent, and if the person stops that job the condition of stay may no longer hold.

Why ImmD cares. Innovation policy increases visas, short-term training and frequent travel. The 2026 Policy Address includes a new short-term training visa. See the news page. The detailed rules follow the visa regulation when it is implemented.

How to use it. If asked whether technology will replace officers, split the answer. Biometrics at the channel are Angle 6. A technology quota inside a talent scheme is Angle 3. Both must land on “a person still judges the exception”.

5　執法5　Enforcement

是甚麼。和入境處最貼的跨境執法，不是海關驗貨，而是人的欺詐和非法停留：假結婚、非法受僱、偽造證件、逾期之後的遣送。五則新聞已在上面展開。

入境處為何關心。大灣區流動越方便，用方便來掩飾假關係、假學歷和假僱主的誘因也越大。情報可以跨邊境，決定准許入境的人仍是口岸上的主任。

怎樣用。一題只抓一個主罪類，不要五個例子堆在一起。假結婚接 S2，黑工接 S1，偽證接 S4，聲請後仍留下接 S5。每題都加一句：與廣東或領事館合作是來源控制，不代替本地的法定程序。

What it is. The cross-boundary enforcement closest to ImmD is not Customs cargo examination. It is fraud and unlawful stay by people: bogus marriage, illegal employment, forged documents, and removal after an overstay. The five news cards above already expand those.

Why ImmD cares. The easier Greater Bay Area movement becomes, the greater the incentive to hide a false relationship, false qualifications or a false employer inside that convenience. Intelligence can cross the boundary. The person who decides whether to grant permission to land is still the officer at the port.

How to use it. Take one main offence per question. Do not stack five examples. Bogus marriage joins S2, illegal employment joins S1, forged documents join S4, and remaining after a claim joins S5. Add one line every time: cooperation with Guangdong or a consulate is control at the source. It does not replace the local statutory procedure.

6　證件與外遊協助6　Travel documents and assistance abroad

是甚麼。特區護照、簽證身份書、回港證服務的是香港居民的旅行身份。年報撮要寫截至 2025 年年底，174 個國家或地區給予特區護照持有人免簽證或落地簽證，引用前對年報。

AHU／1868。協助在外香港居民小組（Assistance to Hong Kong Residents Unit）提供 24 小時熱線 (852) 1868。另有 1868 WhatsApp 求助熱線（發訊息至 (852) 1868；文字、相片、錄音及影片；不支援語音通話）和 1868 微信求助熱線（先關注「香港入境事務處」，再按「1868求助熱線」）。只供身處香港境外、需要援助的居民，不是一般查詢（一般查詢 (852) 2824 6111），也不是口岸檢查。官網：協助在外的香港居民。

入境處為何關心。證件可信，別的地方才願意給免簽；證件被冒用，免簽優惠和口岸信任會一起受損。外遊協助則讓「公共服務」這一層在面試裡站得住，不把入境處說成只有管制站。

怎樣用。答「你對部門有何認識」時，五個服務裡留一個位置給證件和 1868，完整口述用面試 §20.1。不要把護照簽發條件和逗留條件混成同一件事；護照見 Cap.539 的概念層，在職能頁。

What it is. The HKSAR passport, the Document of Identity and the Re-entry Permit serve a Hong Kong resident’s travel identity. The annual-report digest says that by the end of 2025, 174 countries or territories offered visa-free access or visa on arrival to HKSAR passport holders. Check the report before you cite it.

AHU / 1868. The Assistance to Hong Kong Residents Unit runs the 24-hour hotline (852) 1868. There is also a 1868 WhatsApp hotline (message (852) 1868; text, photos, audio and video; no voice calls) and a 1868 WeChat hotline (follow「香港入境事務處」, then tap「1868求助熱線」). It is help for a Hong Kong resident outside Hong Kong who needs assistance. It is not the general enquiry line ((852) 2824 6111), and it is not a control-point check. Official page: Assistance outside Hong Kong.

Why ImmD cares. Other places offer visa-free access because the document is trusted. If the document is misused, the visa-free concession and trust at the port are damaged together. Assistance abroad is what lets “public service” stand up in an interview, so ImmD is not described as control points only.

How to use it. When asked what you know about the department, leave one of the five services for documents and 1868. The full oral answer is interview §20.1. Do not mix passport-issue conditions with conditions of stay. The passport sits at the concept level of Cap. 539, on the knowledge page.

國安／《基本法》第二十三條／境外勢力National security, Basic Law Article 23, external forces

香港國安法，和 2024 年按《基本法》第二十三條制定的《維護國家安全條例》，是兩套法例。不要合成一條，也不要背罰則。

「境外勢力」是國安法例裡的概念，涵蓋外國政府、政黨，以及與之有聯繫的組織或人。考場只說到：身份、資金或關係如果牽涉境外勢力，不要當成普通便利個案。偽證和假文件仍先走 Cap.115 s.42 和 S4，不是每一宗假學歷都是第二十三條。

立場用角度 2。入職測試見基本法及香港國安法測試。部門把自己放在維護國家安全體系裡的一句，見 2026 年 2 月 13 日工作回顧：新聞。

The National Security Law, and the Safeguarding National Security Ordinance made in 2024 under Basic Law Article 23, are two ordinances. Do not merge them, and do not recite penalties.

“External force” is a concept in the national-security legislation. It covers a foreign government, a foreign political party, and an organisation or person connected with them. In the exam, say only this: if identity, money or a relationship involves an external force, do not treat the case as ordinary facilitation. False documents still start at Cap. 115 s. 42 and S4. Not every fake credential is an Article 23 case.

The stance is Angle 2. The entry test is the Basic Law and National Security Law test. The line in which the department places itself inside the national-security system is the work review of 13 February 2026: news.

港車北上，以及假期人流Northbound Travel for Hong Kong Vehicles, and holiday peaks

「港車北上」由 2023 年 7 月 1 日起，讓合資格的香港非商用私家車經港珠澳大橋往來廣東。申請和配額在運輸署計劃頁，本站不背配額。計劃頁：港車北上。

From 1 July 2023, Northbound Travel for Hong Kong Vehicles lets eligible non-commercial Hong Kong private cars travel to Guangdong via the HZMB. Applications and any quota sit on the Transport Department scheme page. This site does not memorise a quota. Scheme page: Northbound Travel.

ImmD’s part is clearance at the HZMB Hong Kong Port. The driver and passengers still clear immigration. A vehicle kiosk is not a lane with no check. Customs deals with goods. ImmD deals with people. Do not swap them.

A single-day peak: for northbound private cars, this site still has no single-day arrival count at the HZMB Hong Kong Port to recite. For the year, across Hong Kong, use only the report’s about 335 million. See passenger flow. Huanggang has separate two-hour measurements. They are not a full-day total, and they are not the northbound-car scheme. See the new Huanggang Port card. Chung Yeung bus numbers and headways are a transport arrangement. See the note. Do not call that an ImmD result. The Halloween extension at Shenzhen Bay is on the Shenzhen Bay card. That release also has no single-day count. NB2 and NB3 are not an ImmD result.

黑工的政策句仍用時事第二則和S1。不要停在加強宣傳、加人手、加罰則。

Dawn (曙光) and Champion (冠軍) are operation names against illegal employment. In the releases, Dawn is usually ImmD’s own operation, and Champion is usually written as joint action with the police.
