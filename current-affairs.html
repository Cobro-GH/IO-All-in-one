演講：印象 1 是「一次放行不是沒有檢查」。一則演講只選下面一個數字。三層見皇崗示例。脈搏頁只作索引：JRE public pulse（p66），數字以公報為準。Speech: impression 1 is “one release is not the absence of a check”. One talk uses only one figure below. 背景。2026 年施政報告寫：重建後的皇崗口岸預計於今年第四季投入服務，實施「一地兩檢」及「合作查驗、一次放行」。那一句是計劃。保安局與深圳市人民政府口岸辦公室 2026 年 10 月 9 日的聯合公布寫落實：根據國務院批覆，皇崗口岸港方口岸區及相關延伸區自 2026 年 7 月 31 日零時起啟用，並依照香港特別行政區法律實施管轄；經粵港協商，口岸於 2026 年 10 月 12 日上午 6 時 30 分正式開通，為旅客和客運車輛提供 24 小時通關，同時暫停往來深圳皇崗口岸臨時旅檢樓和香港落馬洲管制站的旅檢服務。10 月 9 日這份公布沒有重寫「一地兩檢」四個字，也沒有寫設計人次。設計通關流量每日約 20 萬人次、港鐵北環線支線開通後可至每日約 30 萬人次，以及「一地兩檢」「合作查驗、一次放行」，出自 2026 年 7 月 17 日政府歡迎立法會通過《皇崗口岸港方口岸區條例草案》的公布。20 萬和 30 萬是設計流量，不是開通當日的實際人次。

The Top Talent Pass, the Quality Migrant Admission Scheme, admission of Mainland talent, technicians, technology talent and graduates staying on are all about how a person enters lawfully and remains on conditions. The comparison and the common misconceptions are kept in one place, on the identity page.

Why ImmD cares. Bureaux set the direction. ImmD handles the visa, the conditions of stay, genuineness, and enforcement after a breach. The extended trial for graduates of Greater Bay Area campuses is in the Policy Address digest. Do not describe a trial as a permanent rule in an answer.

How to use it. Start with the Angle 3 stance (attract the right talent and prevent abuse), name one feature of one scheme, then use fake credentials or the three-month-early extension as the example. Do not recite a quota unless you checked the official site that day.

3　口岸與新通關模式3　Ports and new clearance modes

是甚麼。e-道、非觸式通道、面上的人面辨識，都是把合資格的人從櫃檯隊伍裡分出去。年報撮要另寫：e-道合資格年齡由 11 歲下調至 7 歲；機場「容易飛 e-道」讓合資格香港居民以容貌識別入境。施政報告撮要寫港珠澳大橋「智通行」先導（車上無感通關），以及重建後皇崗口岸預計第四季實施的「一地兩檢」和「合作查驗、一次放行」。後者的落實是 2026 年 10 月 12 日上午 6 時 30 分的正式開通，出處和可用數字見新皇崗口岸，不要在這一段另背一套人次。「粵艇南下」是另一條便利通道，不是皇崗這種陸地管制站：船的手續在海事處，船上的人仍要過入境檢查，見遊艇卡。年報與施政報告原文在年報與施政報告。

入境處為何關心。新模式改的是檢查怎麼做，不是檢查可不可以不做。兒童、證件失效、系統未能識別、不是已登記的人，仍然要回到人工判斷。

怎樣用。角度 1＋6。私隱用 DPP 的「收集目的、保安、查閱」一句帶過，見法例頁，不要把人面辨識說成沒有法律界限。

What it is. e-Channel, contactless channels and facial recognition take eligible people out of the counter queue. The annual-report digest also says the eligible age for e-Channel was lowered from 11 to 7, and that “Easy Fly e-Channel” at the airport lets eligible Hong Kong residents arrive by facial recognition. The Policy Address digest covers the “Smart Crossing” pilot at the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge (contactless clearance from the vehicle), and co-location with “joint inspection, one release” at the rebuilt Huanggang Port, then expected in the fourth quarter. The opening at 6:30 a.m. on 12 October 2026 is that plan carried out. The sources and the figures you may use are on the new Huanggang Port card. Do not memorise a second passenger total in this paragraph. Southbound travel for Guangdong yachts is another facilitation channel, not a land control point like Huanggang: vessel formalities sit with the Marine Department, and the people on board still clear immigration. See the yacht card. The report and the Policy Address are at the annual report and the Policy Address.

Why ImmD cares. A new mode changes how the check is done, not whether a check may be skipped. A child, an invalid document, a system that cannot identify the person, or someone who is not registered still comes back to a human decision.

How to use it. Angles 1 and 6. For privacy, one DPP line is enough: purpose of collection, security, and access. See the law page. Do not describe facial recognition as having no legal limit.

4　創科4　Innovation and technology

是甚麼。河套、新田一類合作區是人才和科研人員跨境活動的背景，不是入境處去營運園區。與主任直接有關的是 TechTAS 這類配額：合資格科企輸入科技人才，人一旦停止該份工作，逗留條件可能不再成立。

入境處為何關心。創科政策會增加簽證、短期培訓和往來頻密的個案。2026 年施政報告有「新增短期培訓簽證」的方向，見新聞頁，細則以實施時的簽證規例為準。

怎樣用。答「科技會否取代主任」時分開兩層：通道上的生物辨識是角度 6；人才計劃裡的創科配額是角度 3。兩層都要落到「人仍要判斷例外」。

What it is. Cooperation zones such as the Lok Ma Chau Loop and San Tin are the setting for cross-boundary movement of talent and researchers. ImmD does not run the parks. What concerns an officer directly is a quota such as TechTAS: an eligible technology enterprise brings in technology talent, and if the person stops that job the condition of stay may no longer hold.

Why ImmD cares. Innovation policy increases visas, short-term training and frequent travel. The 2026 Policy Address includes a new short-term training visa. See the news page. The detailed rules follow the visa regulation when it is implemented.

How to use it. If asked whether technology will replace officers, split the answer. Biometrics at the channel are Angle 6. A technology quota inside a talent scheme is Angle 3. Both must land on “a person still judges the exception”.

5　執法5　Enforcement

是甚麼。和入境處最貼的跨境執法，不是海關驗貨，而是人的欺詐和非法停留：假結婚、非法受僱、偽造證件、逾期之後的遣送。五則新聞已在上面展開。

入境處為何關心。大灣區流動越方便，用方便來掩飾假關係、假學歷和假僱主的誘因也越大。情報可以跨邊境，決定准許入境的人仍是口岸上的主任。

怎樣用。一題只抓一個主罪類，不要五個例子堆在一起。假結婚接 S2，黑工接 S1，偽證接 S4，聲請後仍留下接 S5。每題都加一句：與廣東或領事館合作是來源控制，不代替本地的法定程序。

What it is. The cross-boundary enforcement closest to ImmD is not Customs cargo examination. It is fraud and unlawful stay by people: bogus marriage, illegal employment, forged documents, and removal after an overstay. The five news cards above already expand those.

Why ImmD cares. The easier Greater Bay Area movement becomes, the greater the incentive to hide a false relationship, false qualifications or a false employer inside that convenience. Intelligence can cross the boundary. The person who decides whether to grant permission to land is still the officer at the port.

How to use it. Take one main offence per question. Do not stack five examples. Bogus marriage joins S2, illegal employment joins S1, forged documents join S4, and remaining after a claim joins S5. Add one line every time: cooperation with Guangdong or a consulate is control at the source. It does not replace the local statutory procedure.

6　證件與外遊協助6　Travel documents and assistance abroad

是甚麼。特區護照、簽證身份書、回港證服務的是香港居民的旅行身份；1868 協助在外港人，是公共服務而不是口岸檢查。年報撮要寫截至 2025 年年底，174 個國家或地區給予特區護照持有人免簽證或落地簽證，引用前對年報。

入境處為何關心。證件可信，別的地方才願意給免簽；證件被冒用，免簽優惠和口岸信任會一起受損。外遊協助則讓「公共服務」這一層在面試裡站得住，不把入境處說成只有管制站。

怎樣用。答「你對部門有何認識」時，五個服務裡留一個位置給證件和 1868，完整口述用面試 §20.1。不要把護照簽發條件和逗留條件混成同一件事；護照見 Cap.539 的概念層，在職能頁。

What it is. The HKSAR passport, the Document of Identity and the Re-entry Permit serve a Hong Kong resident’s travel identity. Assistance to Hong Kong residents abroad on 1868 is a public service, not a control-point check. The annual-report digest says that by the end of 2025, 174 countries or territories offered visa-free access or visa on arrival to HKSAR passport holders. Check the report before you cite it.

Why ImmD cares. Other places offer visa-free access because the document is trusted. If the document is misused, the visa-free concession and trust at the port are damaged together. Assistance abroad is what lets “public service” stand up in an interview, so ImmD is not described as control points only.

How to use it. When asked what you know about the department, leave one of the five services for documents and 1868. The full oral answer is interview §20.1. Do not mix passport-issue conditions with conditions of stay. The passport sits at the concept level of Cap. 539, on the knowledge page.
