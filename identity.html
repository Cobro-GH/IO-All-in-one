身份證符號 A／C／R／U（HD 筆記）Identity-card symbols A / C / R / U (HD notes)

溫習時字母下中英各一行。考場模式只留所選語言。In study mode each letter keeps one Chinese line and one English line. Exam mode keeps only the selected language.

A有居留權right of abode

C登記時逗留受限制stay limited by Director at registration

R有入境權right to land

U登記時逗留不受限制（≠自動等於永久居民，口語誤解要小心）stay not limited by Director at registration

三粒星≠永久居民。真正要會解釋 A／C／R／U 與居留權／入境權差異。Three stars are not the same as permanent resident. You must be able to explain A / C / R / U and the difference between right of abode and right to land.

出生地：Z 香港／X 內地／W 澳門／O 其他。變更：B 出生資料曾改／N 姓名曾改。Place of birth: Z Hong Kong / X Mainland / W Macao / O other. Change: B birth particulars changed / N name changed.

逗留條件 13 類（Landing Slip 筆記清單）13 conditions of stay (landing-slip study list)

HD 下列是備考解釋，不是落地簽證的現行版式。面試時講「類別限制了甚麼活動」，罰則和最新字眼以落地條件及電子法例為準。The notes below are for revision. They are not the current wording of a landing slip. In an interview, say what activity the class limits. Penalties and the latest wording follow the landing condition and the e-Legislation.

  1. Unconditional Landing　無條件准許入境Unconditional landing

    落地時沒有加上限期逗留這一條。它接近「入境權／無條件逗留」的通關結果，不等於身份證上的 A（居留權）。面試一句：無條件逗留的人仍要守其他法律，不要把 U 或無條件落地說成自動永久居民。

    No time limit on stay is imposed on landing. It is close to a clearance result of “right to land / unconditional stay”. It is not symbol A (right of abode) on an identity card. Interview line: a person on unconditional stay must still obey other laws. Do not treat U, or unconditional landing, as automatic permanent residence.

  2. Time Limitation (Residence)　限期居留Time limitation (residence)

    可以在批出的期限內以居留目的留下，到期前要離境或獲延期。就業未必自動准許，要看該次條件有沒有寫明。面試時先讀條件，不要憑「居留」二字假設可以任意工作。

    The person may remain for residence within the period granted, and must leave or obtain an extension before it ends. Employment is not automatically allowed; read whether this condition says so. In an interview, read the condition first. Do not assume from the word “residence” that any work is allowed.

  3. Time Limitation (Dependent)　受養人限期逗留Time limitation (dependant)

    逗留依附保證人的身份和期限。保證人如果不再符合身份，受養人的條件可能一併受影響。面試提醒：受養不等於已經有獨立工作權，能否工作要看當時的受養政策，不要憑記憶硬套。

    The stay depends on the sponsor’s status and limit. If the sponsor no longer qualifies, the dependant’s condition may be affected too. Interview reminder: being a dependant is not an independent right to work. Whether work is allowed follows the dependant policy then in force. Do not force a remembered rule onto the case.

  4. Time Limitation (IANG)　非本地畢業生留／回港安排Time limitation (IANG)

    給合資格畢業生一段時間留港或回港找工作、就業。它接在學生路徑之後，但不是學生簽證的自動延長。常見誤解是「讀過書就可以一直留下」；答法：IANG 有自己的期限和條件，見下方人才比較。

    Eligible graduates are given time to stay or return to look for work and to take up employment. It follows a student path, but it is not an automatic extension of the student visa. A common mistake is “I studied, so I can stay for good”. The answer: IANG has its own limit and conditions. See the talent comparison below.

  5. Time Limitation (Quality Migrant)　優才限期逗留Time limitation (quality migrant)

    對應計分制的優秀人才，通常不是「先有一份本地僱主合約才可申請」的傳統路徑。面試不要把優才說成高才通：一個靠綜合計分，一個靠高收入或指定學歷的快速通道。

    This matches the points-based quality-migrant route. It is usually not the traditional path that starts with a local employment contract. In an interview, do not call QMAS the Top Talent Pass: one uses an overall points score, the other a fast route based on high income or specified qualifications.

  6. Time Limitation (ASSG)　回港第二代的限期逗留Time limitation (ASSG)

    筆記指香港永久性居民的第二代、在外地出生的中國籍人士的入境安排。重點是它有獨立的申請資格和期限，不是憑父母的身份證就自動擁有居留權。資格細節以現行計劃說明為準。

    The notes refer to the admission arrangement for the second generation of Hong Kong permanent residents: Chinese citizens born overseas. The point is that it has its own eligibility and time limit. A parent’s identity card does not automatically confer right of abode. The detail of eligibility follows the current scheme note.

  7. Employment (General)　一般就業Employment (general)

    逗留和獲批的僱主、職位掛鉤。轉換僱主或職位通常要事先批准，否則可能變成違反逗留條件。面試一句：GEP 管的是「這份工作」，不是一張可以隨意打工的許可。

    The stay is tied to the approved employer and post. Changing employer or post usually needs prior approval, or it may become a breach of the condition of stay. Interview line: the General Employment Policy governs “this job”. It is not a permit to take any work.

  8. Student　學生Student

    目的是在獲批院校就讀。一般不可把學生身份當成工作簽證；實習或兼職只在條件或當時政策明文准許時才可以做。違反就同時碰到逗留條件和非法受僱。

    The purpose is study at the approved institution. Student status is not a work visa. An internship or part-time job is allowed only when the condition or the policy then in force says so in writing. A breach touches both the condition of stay and illegal employment.

  9. Student (postsecondary)　專上學生Student (post-secondary)

    HD 筆記標明 w.e.f. 1.6.05，用來和中小學的學生條件分開。專上學生有時獲准有限度的實習或兼職，但限度、週數和要否事先批准會改。答題只說「有特別條件，以落地簽和現行指引為準」，不要背一個過時時數。

    The HD notes mark this “w.e.f. 1.6.05”, to separate it from a school-student condition. Post-secondary students are sometimes allowed limited internship or part-time work, but the limit, the weeks and whether prior approval is required change. In an answer say only “there is a special condition; the landing slip and the current guideline prevail”. Do not recite an outdated number of hours.

  10. Employment (Domestic Helper — Driving not prohibited)　外傭，駕駛未禁止Employment (domestic helper — driving not prohibited)

    受僱於指定僱主做家庭傭工，通常須與僱主同住。這一版沒有加上「不得駕駛」的限制，不代表可以改做司機或外出做另一份工。

    Employment is with a named employer as a domestic helper, and the helper usually must live with that employer. This version does not add a “driving prohibited” limit. That does not mean the person may switch to being a driver or take another job outside.

  11. Employment (Domestic Helper — Driving prohibited)　外傭，禁止駕駛Employment (domestic helper — driving prohibited)

    與上一項同屬外傭，但條件寫明不得駕駛。面試用這對來說明：同一種簽證，落地字眼可以不同；執法看的是這張落地條件，不是類別的俗稱。

    This is the same helper class as the previous item, but the condition states that driving is prohibited. Use the pair in an interview: the same kind of visa can carry different landing words. Enforcement reads this landing condition, not the nickname of the class.

  12. Employment (Imported Worker)　輸入勞工Employment (imported worker)

    按輸入勞工或補充勞工一類配額和合約來港，通常綁定僱主、工種和期限。不是技術專才或高才通的別名。工人不能自行「轉廠」；僱主也不能把人借去合約以外的崗位。

    The person comes under an imported-worker or supplementary-labour quota and contract, usually tied to an employer, a trade and a period. It is not another name for a technician scheme or the Top Talent Pass. The worker cannot “change factory” on their own, and the employer cannot lend the person to a post outside the contract.

  13. Visitor　訪客Visitor

    旅遊、探親、商務訪問等短期目的。一般不可就業、開業或註冊就讀。訪客在港工作，是 s.38AA 和違反逗留條件的典型情景，見法例頁。商務會議不等於在港受僱。

    Tourism, visiting family, a business visit and other short purposes. Employment, starting a business or registering as a student is generally not allowed. A visitor working in Hong Kong is the typical scenario for s. 38AA and a breach of a condition of stay. See the law page. A business meeting is not employment in Hong Kong.

TTPS 高才通TTPS — Top Talent Pass

通常不必先有僱主。目的是讓高收入或指定高學歷的人更快進來。面試一句：平衡吸引與真確性把關。

申請要旨。看的是申請人本人的收入或學歷類別，批出後仍要按該次逗留條件生活，續簽再審當時的規定。

常見誤解。高才通不是居留權，也不是「文件交了就不會被查」。偽造學歷案說明審查存在；2026 年施政報告對部分初創科企續簽寬免收入證明，只限指定支持的經營者，見時事第五則。

An employer is usually not required first. The purpose is a faster route for people with a high income or specified high qualifications. Interview line: balance attraction with a genuineness check.

What the application turns on. It looks at the applicant’s own income or qualification class. After approval the person still lives under that condition of stay, and renewal is assessed against the rules then in force.

Common misconception. The Top Talent Pass is not right of abode, and lodging documents does not mean they will not be checked. The fake-credential case shows that checking exists. The 2026 Policy Address waiver of some income proof on renewal is only for operators of designated supported start-ups. See news card 5.

QMAS 優才QMAS — Quality Migrant Admission Scheme

計分制，不是傳統的「先拿到聘書」。目的是吸納優秀人才，和人口、經濟結構有關。

申請要旨。綜合條件達標後獲限期逗留，不是高才通的收入快速通道。

常見誤解。分數高不等於免核對身份和文件，也不等於可以忽略逗留期限。

It is a points scheme, not the traditional “offer letter first” path. The purpose is to admit quality talent, linked to population and the structure of the economy.

What the application turns on. A person who meets the overall conditions is given a limited stay. It is not the Top Talent Pass income fast track.

Common misconception. A high score does not skip identity and document checks, and it does not mean the time limit can be ignored.

GEP 一般就業GEP — General Employment Policy

通常要有本地聘用。逗留條件與該份工作掛鉤。

申請要旨。僱主、崗位和期限是條件的一部分。轉換工作一般要事先獲准。

常見誤解。「已在港工作」不等於可以隨意跳槽；未獲准就轉僱主，可能變成違反逗留條件。

There is usually a local employment. The condition of stay is tied to that job.

What the application turns on. The employer, the post and the period are part of the condition. Changing job generally needs prior permission.

Common misconception. “Already working in Hong Kong” does not mean the person may change employer at will. Changing employer without permission may be a breach of the condition of stay.

ASMTP 輸入內地人才ASMTP — Admission Scheme for Mainland Talents and Professionals

通常有聘用，用來補充本地短缺的專才，不是所有內地來港的人都走這一條。

申請要旨。和 GEP 一樣強調真實聘用，但對象和計劃目的不同，答題時不要併成「專才」一個詞。

常見誤解。單程證定居、雙程證探訪、ASMTP 工作，是三套制度。

There is usually an employment, to fill a local shortage of professionals. Not everyone from the Mainland uses this route.

What the application turns on. Like the GEP it stresses genuine employment, but the people and the purpose differ. Do not collapse them into one word, “professionals”, in an answer.

Common misconception. Settlement on a One-way Permit, a visit on an Exit-Entry Permit, and work under ASMTP are three systems.

技術專才Technicians

有配額或工種清單的框架，對應短缺技術工種，把人才政策從高學歷擴到技術專業。

申請要旨。工種、配額和薪酬一類條件是計劃的一部分，背數目前先查現行清單。

常見誤解。不是訪客可以先開工、再補手續，也不是外傭或輸入勞工的改名。

A quota or a list of trades, aimed at technical trades in shortage. It extends talent policy from high academic qualifications to technical professions.

What the application turns on. Trade, quota and pay-type conditions are part of the scheme. Check the current list before you memorise a number.

Common misconception. It is not “a visitor may start work and file the papers later”, and it is not a renamed domestic-helper or imported-worker scheme.

TechTAS

給合資格科企的配額，用來輸入創科人才。終止該份工作後要留意身份還在不在。

申請要旨。公司和崗位都要符合當時的科企資格，人的逗留跟著這項輸入安排。

常見誤解。科企配額不是個人自己申請的高才通，離職後不會自動變成無條件逗留。

A quota for eligible technology enterprises, used to bring in innovation and technology talent. If that job ends, check whether the status still holds.

What the application turns on. Both the company and the post must meet the technology-enterprise rules then in force. The person’s stay follows that admission arrangement.

Common misconception. An enterprise quota is not a Top Talent Pass the individual applies for alone. Leaving the job does not automatically become unconditional stay.

IANG

非本地畢業生留港或回港就業的安排，與學生簽證銜接，但是另一段許可。

申請要旨。先符合畢業與院校條件，才獲得找工作或就業的期限。大灣區校園畢業生的試行延長見施政報告撮要，不要說成所有分校畢業生都已永久納入。

常見誤解。學生簽證期間的有限度實習，不等於 IANG；IANG 也不等於已經擁有居留權。

The arrangement for non-local graduates to stay or return for employment. It follows a student visa, but it is a separate permission.

What the application turns on. The person must first meet the graduation and institution conditions, and only then receives time to look for work or to take up employment. The extended trial for Greater Bay Area campus graduates is in the Policy Address digest. Do not say every branch-campus graduate has been brought in permanently.

Common misconception. Limited internship during a student visa is not IANG, and IANG is not right of abode.

其他易混概念Other easy mix-ups

  • DI 簽證身份書。香港居民不一定人人持有特區護照；不能或不以中國公民身份領護照的人，可以用簽證身份書作旅行證件。答題時把 DI 放在「旅行證件」一層，不要把它說成簽證或居留權。
  • 單程證和雙程證。單程證是內地主管部門簽發、來港定居的安排；雙程證是往來探訪。兩者都不是入境處的高才通或工作簽證。面試只要分清「定居」和「探訪」，不要背內地簽發細節。
  • CoE 居留權證明書。聲稱擁有居留權、但人還未以這個身份入境的人，要先經證明書審核，獲發後才按該身份入境。一句記住：先審核，後入境，不是在櫃檯口頭自稱永久居民即可。
  • Arrest、detain、removal、deportation。Arrest 是刑事逮捕；detain 在入境語境常指法定的行政羈留（等候審查或查訊）；removal 是遣送離境；deportation 是遞解離境。四個詞對應不同權力、職級和後果，情景題裡不要互換。
  • APIS。預報旅客資料讓風險在飛機落地前就被標出來，檢查從「人走到櫃檯才開始看」變成「源頭已經有名單」。它加強預判，不取代落地時的身份核對和拒絕入境權。
  • DI, Document of Identity. Not every Hong Kong resident holds an HKSAR passport. A person who cannot, or does not, obtain a passport as a Chinese citizen may use a Document of Identity as a travel document. In an answer put the DI at the “travel document” layer. Do not call it a visa or right of abode.
  • One-way Permit and Exit-Entry Permit. The One-way Permit is issued by the Mainland authority for settlement in Hong Kong. The Exit-Entry Permit (often called the two-way permit) is for visits. Neither is ImmD’s Top Talent Pass or a work visa. In an interview, separate “settlement” from “visit”. Do not recite Mainland issuance detail.
  • CoE, Certificate of Entitlement. A person who claims right of abode but has not yet entered in that status must first be verified for the certificate, and only enters in that status after it is issued. One line: verification first, entry second. Saying “I am a permanent resident” at the counter is not enough.
  • Arrest, detain, removal, deportation. Arrest is a criminal arrest. In the immigration context, detain often means statutory administrative detention while awaiting examination or an inquiry. Removal is removal from Hong Kong. Deportation is a deportation order. The four words match different powers, ranks and consequences. Do not swap them in a scenario.
  • APIS. Advance passenger information flags risk before the aircraft lands. The check moves from “we start looking when the person reaches the counter” to “the source already has a list”. It strengthens anticipation. It does not replace an identity check on landing, or the power to refuse entry.
