形式Format

  • 小組討論之後隨即進行。語言是全英語。
  • 抽一個抽象英文詞（例如 Resilience、Integrity、Connectivity、Gatekeeper、Innovation），用它講一個公共服務判斷。
  • 零準備。沒有 12 秒、沒有 1 分鐘構思。抽到就開口。
  • 長度約 2 分 30 秒。目標是在 2:15–2:30 之間收完，不要 1 分鐘就停，也不要衝過 2:30。
  • 考官看四件事：冷靜（不僵住）、結構（五段）、英語信心、主任級領導（判斷留在人，不把責任交給機器或群眾）。
  • It follows the group discussion at once. The language is English throughout.
  • You draw one abstract English word (Resilience, Integrity, Connectivity, Gatekeeper, Innovation, and others). Use it for one judgement in public service.
  • No preparation. Not 12 seconds, and not a minute to plan. You start when you draw.
  • About 2 minutes 30 seconds. Finish between 2:15 and 2:30. Do not stop at one minute, and do not run past 2:30.
  • Four things: coolness (you do not freeze), structure (the five blocks), confidence in English, and officer-level leadership (judgement stays with a person; it is not handed to a machine or to the crowd).

1-2-3 印象：由淺到深的三層The 1-2-3 impression: three layers, shallow to deep

考官幾分鐘後通常只記得三層。先給一句可以複述的話，再給一個證據，最後把那一句說成你是怎樣的主任。時鐘仍是上面五段。

印象 1｜幾秒內的第一印象。一句話，聽完可以複述。不要數字，不要故事細節，不要「首先、其次、最後」。這句必須用到題目裡的詞。例：抽到 Adaptability，就說「適應力是改方法，不改責任」。

印象 2｜結構和證據。只放一個場景，或一個已經在本站出現的官方數字，加上入境處角度。個人故事用 A 或 B。公共例子用下面的年報、施政報告或時事卡，口頭要說出處：「年報序言撮要」「2026 年施政報告」「角度筆記」。一則演講只講一個數字。講不出出處就不要講那個數字。

印象 3｜更深的價值和收結。把印象 1 說成主任的身份：依法、有禮、判斷沒有交給機器或交給群眾。最後一句回到印象 1，用更深的詞再說一次，讓人帶走。

任何題都同一條路，但時鐘以五段為準。用題目造印象 1，放在 0:00–0:25。印象 2 拆開：0:25–1:15 是社會，1:15–1:55 是入境處，只選一張來源卡。印象 3 放在 1:55–2:20，2:20–2:30 把印象 1 再說一次。不要在中段換題。Drive 舊鐘（約 0:00–0:40 開場、最後 40 秒收結、總長約 3 分鐘）長過考場，只可當加長排練。

A few minutes later the examiner usually remembers only three layers. Give one line they can repeat, then one piece of evidence, then say that line as the kind of officer you would be. The clock is still the five blocks above.

Impression 1, the first few seconds. One sentence the listener can repeat. No figure, no story detail, no “first, second, finally”. The sentence must use the word in the question. If the word is adaptability: “Adaptability means changing the method, not the duty.”

Impression 2, structure and evidence. One scene, or one official figure that is already on this site, plus the ImmD angle. For a personal scene use story A or B. For a public scene use the annual report, the Policy Address or a current-affairs card below, and name the source aloud: “the annual-report foreword digest”, “the 2026 Policy Address”, or “the angles notes”. One talk, one figure. If you cannot name the source, do not say the figure.

Impression 3, the deeper value and the close. Say impression 1 as an officer’s identity: according to law, with courtesy, and with judgement that is not handed to a machine or to the crowd. The last sentence returns to impression 1 in deeper words, so that line is what they keep.

Every topic uses the same path, and the clock is the five blocks. Build impression 1 at 0:00–0:25. Split impression 2: society at 0:25–1:15, ImmD at 1:15–1:55, one source card only. Impression 3 sits at 1:55–2:20, and 2:20–2:30 says impression 1 again. Do not change the question in the middle. Drive’s old clock (about 0:00–0:40 to open, the last 40 seconds to close, about 3 minutes in all) is longer than the exam. Use it only as a longer drill.

把年報和施政報告說成印象Turn the annual report and the Policy Address into impressions

新聞頁的年報是八點撮要，施政報告很多則原先只有同一句「應考提示」，沒有告訴你這句話在演講的哪一層。下面每則都標：它是甚麼意思、入境處角度、落在印象幾、以及約 20–40 秒可以怎麼講。數字只來自年報撮要、施政報告、五年規劃或時事卡。同一則演講只用其中一個數字。

The annual report on the news page is eight digest points, and many Policy Address items had only the same exam tip, with no hint of which speech layer they belong to. Each example below says what it means, the ImmD angle, which impression it fills, and a bite of about 20–40 seconds. Figures come only from the annual-report digest, the Policy Address, the five-year plan, or the current-affairs cards. Use only one figure in a single talk.

約 30 秒

年報序言的撮要說，2025 年出入境約 3.35 億人次，比 2024 年多約 12%，也超過 2019 年約 3 億的紀錄。人多了，檢查沒有取消：e-道年齡由 11 歲降到 7 歲，機場「容易飛 e-道」讓合資格居民用容貌識別，不用再出示實體證件。我的第一句是便利給合資格的人；最後一句是判斷仍在主任手上。正式講之前我會再對年報原文。

What it means. A record does not mean the check can be skipped. The report puts passenger flow and self-service clearance in the same passage. The test is whether you only recite the headcount.

ImmD angle. Eligible people move faster. A doubtful case stops. e-Channel and facial recognition are streaming, not a transfer of judgement to a machine.

Which layer. Impression 1 has no figure. Impression 2 uses only one figure below; leave the rest on the news page. Impression 3 is where the officer’s judgement appears.

Figures you may use (one only). In 2025 Hong Kong recorded about 335 million passenger arrivals and departures, about 12% more than in 2024, above the 2019 record of about 300 million. Or: the eligible age for e-Channel was lowered from 11 to 7. Or: at the airport, “Easy Fly e-Channel” lets eligible Hong Kong residents verify identity by facial recognition on arrival, without a physical document or a contactless QR code. A fifth Skytrax award for airport immigration service can be a light closing line. It is not evidence of enforcement.

About 30 seconds

The annual-report foreword digest says that in 2025 there were about 335 million passenger movements, about 12% more than in 2024, and above the 2019 record of about 300 million. More people did not cancel the check. The e-Channel age went from 11 to 7, and Easy Fly e-Channel lets eligible residents clear by facial recognition without producing a physical document. My first line is that facilitation is for eligible people. My last line is that judgement stays with the officer. I would check the report before saying this in the exam.

示例　新皇崗口岸：兩邊查完，一次放行Example — the new Huanggang Port: both sides finish the check, then one release

出處要分開Keep the sources apart 施政報告的「預計第四季」見政策撮要。開通時刻、24 小時旅客和客運車輛、暫停落馬洲旅檢，見 2026-10-09 公報。每日約 20 萬／北環線支線後約 30 萬是 2026-07-17 公布的設計流量。開通當日早上 5,170 人次和 953 架次見 政府新聞網 2026-10-12。10 月 19 日下午約 9,401 人、上升 366% 見 保安局公報。完整卡在時事頁。適合 Cooperation、Change，以及口岸題。不要和年報 3.35 億放在同一則。“Expected in the fourth quarter” is the Policy Address digest. The opening time, 24-hour clearance of passengers and passenger vehicles, and the Lok Ma Chau suspension are the announcement of 9 October 2026. About 200,000 a day, and about 300,000 after the Northern Link spur line, are the design flow in the announcement of 17 July 2026. The opening-morning 5,170 passenger trips and 953 vehicle trips are news.gov.hk, 12 October 2026. About 9,401 people and the rise of 366% on the afternoon of 19 October are the Security Bureau release. The full card is on the current-affairs page. Use for cooperation, change, and a port question. Do not put it in the same talk as the annual report’s 335 million.

生詞：不要僵住A cold word: do not freeze

  1. 立刻開口。沉默就是失分。第一句可以是：The word I have is ____. I take it to mean ____. 定義稍偏，也好過冷場。不要改背一篇誠信稿。
  2. 看詞根和詞綴。un-／dis- 是相反；-ity／-ness 是一種品質；-tion 是一件做法。Accountability 是 account + ability：你要能解釋這個決定。Gatekeeper 是 gate + keeper：誰可以過、誰要停。
  3. 折回熟悉的政策價值。生詞最後都落到便利與保安、誠信、服務，或守門。說得出「如果我當值」就夠，不要裝懂詞源全書。
  1. Start at once. Silence is the loss. The first line can be: “The word I have is ____. I take it to mean ____.” A slightly off definition is better than a freeze. Do not switch to a memorised integrity script.
  2. Use the root and the affix. Un- / dis- reverse it. -ity / -ness name a quality. -tion names a practice. Accountability is account + ability: you can explain the decision. Gatekeeper is gate + keeper: who may pass, and who must stop.
  3. Reframe onto a policy value you already know. A cold word lands on facilitation and security, integrity, service, or keeping the gate. “If I were on duty” is enough. Do not pretend to know the whole etymology.
