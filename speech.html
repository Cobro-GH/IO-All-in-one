1-2-3 印象：由淺到深The 1-2-3 impression, shallow to deep

即席演講不是背時間軸，也不是把年報數字念完。考官幾分鐘後還記得的，通常只有三層。先給最淺的一句，再給一個證據，最後把那一句說成你是怎樣的主任。

印象 1｜幾秒內的第一印象。一句話，聽完可以複述。不要數字，不要故事細節，不要「首先、其次、最後」。這句必須用到題目裡的詞。例：抽到 Adaptability，就說「適應力是改方法，不改責任」。

印象 2｜結構和證據。只放一個場景，或一個已經在本站出現的官方數字，加上入境處角度。個人故事用 A 或 B。公共例子用下面的年報、施政報告或時事卡，口頭要說出處：「年報序言撮要」「2026 年施政報告」「角度筆記」。一則演講只講一個數字。講不出出處就不要講那個數字。

印象 3｜更深的價值和收結。把印象 1 說成主任的身份：依法、有禮、判斷沒有交給機器或交給群眾。最後一句回到印象 1，用更深的詞再說一次，讓人帶走。

任何題都同一條路。抽到價值詞、時事或「你對部門有多認識」，都是：用題目造印象 1 → 選故事 A／B 或一張來源卡做印象 2 → 用「如果我當值……」做印象 3。不要在中段換題。Drive 的時鐘可以這樣對：約 0:00–0:40 印象 1；中段印象 2；最後約 40 秒印象 3。

An impromptu talk is not a memorised timeline, and it is not a recital of annual-report figures. A few minutes later the examiner usually still holds only three layers. Give the shallowest line first, then one piece of evidence, then say that line as the kind of officer you would be.

Impression 1, the first few seconds. One sentence the listener can repeat. No figure, no story detail, no “first, second, finally”. The sentence must use the word in the question. If the word is adaptability: “Adaptability means changing the method, not the duty.”

Impression 2, structure and evidence. One scene, or one official figure that is already on this site, plus the ImmD angle. For a personal scene use story A or B. For a public scene use the annual report, the Policy Address or a current-affairs card below, and name the source aloud: “the annual-report foreword digest”, “the 2026 Policy Address”, or “the angles notes”. One talk, one figure. If you cannot name the source, do not say the figure.

Impression 3, the deeper value and the close. Say impression 1 as an officer’s identity: according to law, with courtesy, and with judgement that is not handed to a machine or to the crowd. The last sentence returns to impression 1 in deeper words, so that line is what they keep.

Every topic uses the same path. A value word, a news item, or “how well do you know the department?” is the same sequence: build impression 1 from the question, choose story A or B or one source card for impression 2, then “if I were on duty…” for impression 3. Do not change the question in the middle. Drive’s clock can sit on top: about 0:00–0:40 for impression 1, the middle for impression 2, and about the last 40 seconds for impression 3.

Impression 1. A team is not the group that is complete. It is the group that still delivers when someone falls.
Impression 2. In my final year a large project had a very tight deadline, a core member suddenly fell ill, and morale dropped. I called the meeting again, split the work by strengths, broke it into smaller tasks, and listened before deciding. Teamwork, leadership, stress and communication from the Drive outline all sit in this one scene. Do not turn them into four stories.
Impression 3. A control point is also a team. When a passenger is upset, a colleague is unwell, or the queue grows, I will not finish it alone, and I will not wait in silence for an instruction. Close on impression 1: it is a team only if it still is one when it is hard.

印象 2。繁忙餐廳兼職，高峰時顧客因為延遲當眾喝罵，廚房又缺人。我先停一停，聽完，能做的立刻做，不合理的折扣照規定拒絕。這是 Drive 大綱的 EQ、patience、rules、integrity、adaptability，全部在同一分鐘。

1-2-3

Impression 1. Service means staying professional while being shouted at. The rule does not change because the other person is loud.
Impression 2. In a busy restaurant, at the peak, a customer shouted in public about a delay, and the kitchen was short of people. I paused, listened, did at once what I could, and refused a discount the rules did not allow. EQ, patience, rules, integrity and adaptability from the Drive outline all sit in that same minute.
Impression 3. A counter also meets people who are loud, in a hurry, or asking for an exception. Courtesy is the deeper form of impression 1: politeness is not a concession, and keeping the rule is not rudeness. Thank them, and return to “the manner and the rule stay together”.

1-2-3

印象 1。科技是應變工具，不是取消檢查。
印象 2（Drive，無新數字）。疫情打亂熟悉的通關。入境處用無接觸 e-道一類措施，讓合資格的人少接觸，身份仍然核對。口罩、檢疫、跨部門合作都說明同一句：改的是步驟。本站沒有為這則另造人數，所以不要在這裡加統計。

1-2-3

Impression 1. Technology is a tool for disruption. It is not the cancellation of the check.
Impression 2 (Drive, no new figure). The pandemic broke the familiar clearance steps. Contactless e-Channel and similar measures let eligible people pass with less contact while identity was still checked. Masks, quarantine and cross-department work all say the same thing: the steps changed. This site does not add a headcount for that scene, so do not invent one here.
Impression 2 when you have a figure. If the word is technology, or the question is “how well do you know the department?”, switch to the annual-report flow or the Policy Address Smart Crossing item. Saying both becomes a list.
Impression 3. Rules at a control point can still change overnight. I will change the method: another stream, another request for advice. I will not change the identity check, or slow down for a passenger who needs help. Adaptability means changing the method, not the principle.

把年報和施政報告說成印象Turn the annual report and the Policy Address into impressions

新聞頁的年報是八點撮要，施政報告很多則原先只有同一句「應考提示」，沒有告訴你這句話在演講的哪一層。下面每則都標：它是甚麼意思、入境處角度、落在印象幾、以及約 20–40 秒可以怎麼講。數字只來自年報撮要、施政報告、五年規劃或時事卡。同一則演講只用其中一個數字。

The annual report on the news page is eight digest points, and many Policy Address items had only the same exam tip, with no hint of which speech layer they belong to. Each example below says what it means, the ImmD angle, which impression it fills, and a bite of about 20–40 seconds. Figures come only from the annual-report digest, the Policy Address, the five-year plan, or the current-affairs cards. Use only one figure in a single talk.

示例一　年報：人多了，檢查還在Example 1 — annual report: more people, the check remains

出處Source 2025 年年報序言撮要。適合 Technology、Service、Change，以及「你對入境處有多認識」。2025 annual-report foreword digest. Use for technology, service, change, and “how well do you know ImmD?”.

意思。紀錄破了，不代表檢查可以省。年報把「人流」和「自助通關」放在同一段，考的是你會不會只背人次。

入境處角度。合資格的人走得快，有疑問的人停下來。e-道和容貌識別是分流，不是把判斷交給機器。

落在哪一層。印象 1 不要數字。印象 2 只選下面一個數字，其餘留在新聞頁。印象 3 才說主任的判斷。

可用數字（只講一個）。2025 年全港總出入境人次約 3.35 億，較 2024 年升約 12%，並打破 2019 年約 3 億人次的紀錄。或者：e-道合資格年齡由 11 歲下調至 7 歲。或者：機場「容易飛 e-道」，合資格香港居民入境時以容貌識別核實身份，無須出示實體證件或掃描二維碼。Skytrax 第五次「全球最佳機場出入境服務」只可作收結的輕一句，不要當成執法證據。

約 30 秒

年報序言的撮要說，2025 年出入境約 3.35 億人次，比 2024 年多約 12%，也超過 2019 年約 3 億的紀錄。人多了，檢查沒有取消：e-道年齡由 11 歲降到 7 歲，機場「容易飛 e-道」讓合資格居民用容貌識別，不用再出示實體證件。我的第一句是便利給合資格的人；最後一句是判斷仍在主任手上。正式講之前我會再對年報原文。

What it means. A record does not mean the check can be skipped. The report puts passenger flow and self-service clearance in the same passage. The test is whether you only recite the headcount.

ImmD angle. Eligible people move faster. A doubtful case stops. e-Channel and facial recognition are streaming, not a transfer of judgement to a machine.

Which layer. Impression 1 has no figure. Impression 2 uses only one figure below; leave the rest on the news page. Impression 3 is where the officer’s judgement appears.

Figures you may use (one only). In 2025 Hong Kong recorded about 335 million passenger arrivals and departures, about 12% more than in 2024, above the 2019 record of about 300 million. Or: the eligible age for e-Channel was lowered from 11 to 7. Or: at the airport, “Easy Fly e-Channel” lets eligible Hong Kong residents verify identity by facial recognition on arrival, without a physical document or a contactless QR code. A fifth Skytrax award for airport immigration service can be a light closing line. It is not evidence of enforcement.

About 30 seconds

The annual-report foreword digest says that in 2025 there were about 335 million passenger movements, about 12% more than in 2024, and above the 2019 record of about 300 million. More people did not cancel the check. The e-Channel age went from 11 to 7, and Easy Fly e-Channel lets eligible residents clear by facial recognition without producing a physical document. My first line is that facilitation is for eligible people. My last line is that judgement stays with the officer. I would check the report before saying this in the exam.

示例二　施政報告：開門和把關是同一句Example 2 — Policy Address: opening the door and keeping the gate are one sentence

出處Source 2026 年施政報告＋年報「十五五」一句＋時事卡。三個出處不要合成一個數字。The 2026 Policy Address, one line in the annual report on the 15th Five-Year Plan, and the current-affairs card. Do not merge the three sources into one figure.

意思。施政報告的人才段落是「便利誰、便利到哪裡」，不是「審查取消了」。年報只說入境處配合人才政策和電子簽證，以及國家規劃支持香港作為國際高端人才集聚的方向。逾 23 萬人和首次偽造學歷高才通，是角度筆記的易記卡，不是施政報告原文。

入境處角度。主任不決定香港要不要人才。主任核對這個人是否符合該計劃的文件和條件。

落在哪一層。印象 1：「開門和把關是同一句。」印象 2 只講一項施政措施。印象 3 才接到假學歷或逾 23 萬，而且要改口說「角度筆記」，否則聽起來像你在施政報告裡見到一個本站沒有寫進施政報告的數字。

可講的措施（只講一項）。大灣區校園畢業生納入留港／回港就業安排的試行延長兩年，不是所有分校畢業生已經永久納入。或者：獲指定公營機構支持的初創科企經營者，高才通續簽可獲豁免公司收入證明等要求。這和 2026 年 3 月起延長申請可提早三個月不是同一件事，見時事第五則。特邀人士計劃由東盟擴到中亞和中東、以及新的短期培訓簽證，都是「有邀請或有課程仍然要查條件」，不要講成免審查。

約 35 秒

我的第一句是：開門和把關是同一句。施政報告把大灣區校園畢業生留港或回港就業的試行再延長兩年，這是試行，不是所有分校畢業生已經永久納入。另一項要分開記的，是指定支持的初創科企經營者，高才通續簽可以少交一類收入證明；那不是 3 月起提早三個月交延長申請。年報只說到配合人才政策和電子簽證。角度筆記另有逾 23 萬人，以及首次偽造學歷的高才通個案，我不會加案人數或判刑。如果我當值，真確的申請通過，虛假的文件停下。

What it means. The talent paragraphs in the Policy Address say who is facilitated, and how far. They do not say the check is cancelled. The annual report only says ImmD supports the talent-admission policies and electronic visas, and that the national plan supports Hong Kong as a hub for high-end international talent. “More than 230,000” and the first fake-credential Top Talent Pass case are the angles memory card, not the text of the Policy Address.

ImmD angle. An officer does not decide whether Hong Kong wants talent. An officer checks whether this person meets the documents and conditions of that scheme.

Which layer. Impression 1: “Opening the door and keeping the gate are one sentence.” Impression 2 states only one Policy Address measure. Impression 3 may add the fake-credential case or the 230,000, but you must say “the angles notes”. Otherwise it sounds as if that figure appeared in the Policy Address.

Measures you may use (one only). The trial that includes graduates of Hong Kong universities’ Greater Bay Area campuses in the arrangements for non-local graduates is extended for two years. It is not a permanent rule for every branch-campus graduate. Or: operators of start-ups supported by designated public organisations may be exempted from requirements such as proof of company income when they renew a Top Talent Pass. That is not the same thing as applying three months early to extend a stay from March 2026. See current-affairs item 5. The invited-persons scheme, extended from ASEAN to Central Asia and the Middle East, and the new short-term training visa, still mean “an invitation or a course is not a waiver of the conditions”.

About 35 seconds

My first line is that opening the door and keeping the gate are one sentence. The Policy Address extends for two years the trial under which graduates of Hong Kong universities’ Greater Bay Area campuses may stay or return to work. It is a trial, not a permanent rule for every branch-campus graduate. A separate item: operators of designated supported technology start-ups may submit one less class of income proof on Top Talent Pass renewal. That is not the March change that lets people apply three months early to extend a stay. The annual report only says ImmD supports the talent policies and electronic visas. The angles notes add “more than 230,000” and the first fake-credential Top Talent Pass case. I would not add a headcount or a sentence. If I were on duty, a genuine application would proceed and a false document would stop.

示例三　年報遣返＋施政報告打黑工Example 3 — removals in the report, and illegal employment in the Policy Address

出處Source 年報「逾 2,600 人、約 +18%」見刊物；五項打黑工見施政報告；僱主罰款 50 萬元及監禁 10 年是角度筆記，不要說成年報寫的。“More than 2,600 people, about +18%” is the annual report. The five illegal-employment measures are the Policy Address. The employer’s maximum fine of $500,000 and 10 years is the angles note. Do not attribute it to the report.

意思。年報那個數字是「免遣返聲請不獲確立之後的遣離」，不是聲請成功率，也不能證明每一個聲請人都在濫用。施政報告的五項是減低非法受僱的誘因：強化跨部門專責組、業界反黑工大使、網上巡邏、檢視刑罰、針對性聯合行動。兩段回答的是不同的題，硬接在一起要先說「這是兩份文件」。

入境處角度。限期逗留如果沒有執行，條件就只是一句話。執行要快、有記錄，同時不把仍在程序中的人說成已經失敗。

落在哪一層。印象 1 不報 2,600。印象 2 在「遣返」和「黑工」裡只選一個。印象 3 把尊嚴和本地就業優先放回同一句。

約 35 秒

條件如果沒有執行，就只是一句話。年報撮要寫 2025 年遣離超過 2,600 名聲請不獲確立的人士，約比前一年多 18%。這只說明不獲確立之後的執行，我不會用它說所有聲請都是濫用。如果題目是黑工，我就改講施政報告的五項：專責組、業界大使、網上巡邏、檢視刑罰、聯合行動。角度筆記另記僱主最高可罰 50 萬元及監禁 10 年，那是另一張卡。如果我當值，我會依法、盡快、有記錄，也會把遣送和遞解分開。

What it means. The report’s figure is removal after a non-refoulement claim was not substantiated. It is not a success rate for claims, and it does not prove that every claimant is abusing the system. The Policy Address lists five measures that reduce the incentive to employ people illegally: strengthen the inter-departmental task force, appoint trade ambassadors, patrol online, review penalties, and run targeted joint operations. They answer different questions. If you join them, say first that they are two documents.

ImmD angle. A condition of stay that is not enforced is only a sentence. Enforcement should be prompt and on the record, without describing a person who is still in the process as someone who has already failed.

Which layer. Impression 1 does not quote 2,600. Impression 2 chooses either removal or illegal employment, not both figures. Impression 3 puts dignity and local workers’ priority back in one sentence.

About 35 seconds
