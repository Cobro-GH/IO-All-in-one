形式（公開資料＋Drive）

  • Drive 目標：約 3 分鐘；用故事扣入境處相關價值，勿 100% 只講個人。
  • 新聞／培訓機構：小組討論後即席演講；或抽英文詞、零準備、約 2.5–3 分鐘——以當日指示為準。
  • 公開資料未明確 官方招聘頁未必刊準確秒數。

時間節奏（Drive）

  • 建議主體約 45–60 秒／故事；整段約 3 分鐘。
  • 常用結構：2 個個人故事大綱 + 1 個社會／部門例子，清楚扣題（價值詞）。

故事大綱 A — The Group Project

Drive 大綱

開場：大學最後一年帶大型小組專題，deadline 極緊，核心成員突然生病，團隊士氣低落。
轉折：Teamwork／Leadership — 重開會、按強項分工、鼓勵隊員；真正團隊在困難時互相支援。
Stress — 拆細任務、一次一題；冷靜理性對 IO 重要。
Communication — 先聽各方再決策；積極聆聽達共識。

English outline

Opening: In my final year I led a large group project. The deadline was extremely tight, a core member suddenly fell ill, and morale dropped.
Turn: Teamwork / Leadership — we met again, split work by strengths, and encouraged each other; a real team supports one another under pressure.
Stress — break the work into smaller tasks, one issue at a time; calm reasoning matters for an IO.
Communication — listen to every side before deciding; active listening builds consensus.

故事大綱 B — The Part-time Job

Drive 大綱

開場：繁忙餐廳兼職，高峰時顧客因延遲當眾喝罵。
EQ／Patience／Service — 深呼吸、專業、聆聽道歉並迅速處理。
Rules／Integrity — 不合理折扣要求下仍守政策。
Adaptability — 同時應付怒客與廚房人手不足。

English outline

Opening: I worked part-time in a busy restaurant. At peak a customer shouted at me in public because of a delay.
EQ / Patience / Service — breathe, stay professional, listen, apologise, and fix it quickly.
Rules / Integrity — when asked for an unreasonable discount, still follow the policy.
Adaptability — handle an angry customer and a short-staffed kitchen at the same time.

故事大綱 C — COVID／Tech（社會）

Drive 大綱

疫情衝擊邊境與生活。
Technology — ImmD 推無接觸 e-Channel 等。
Change／Adaptability — 口罩、在家工作；靈活求存。
Law enforcement／Rules — 嚴格檢疫；依法執法是穩定基礎。

English outline

The pandemic hit the border and daily life.
Technology — ImmD introduced contactless e-Channel and similar measures.
Change / Adaptability — masks and working from home; flexibility to get through.
Law enforcement / Rules — strict quarantine; enforcing the law is the basis of stability.

完整 3 分鐘結構示例（主題：Adaptability）

  1. 0:00–0:40 Intro：Adaptability 是基本價值；適者生存；對成長與事業重要。
  2. 0:40–1:30 Body 1：Story B（侍應／廚房）→ think on my feet。
  3. 1:30–2:20 Body 2：過渡社會 → Story C（疫情＋e-Channel）→ 城市要持續創新。
  4. 2:20–3:00 Conclusion：改變是唯一常數；立志做 IO，管制站每天突發；承諾保持靈活、持續學習、適應情況服務市民。

Action Plan（Drive）

  1. 預先準備 3 個故事骨架，對應不同價值詞。
  2. 練習自信句：這讓我明白，自信來自準備……This taught me that confidence comes from preparation…
  3. 控時：講太快則在故事中段加細節（顧客表情、隊友情緒）作 padding。

禁忌（網上攻略共識）

  • 抽到不懂就改念預背「誠信講稿」答非所問
  • 重複剛完成的小組討論內容
  • 長時間 cold air
