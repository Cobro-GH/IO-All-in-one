形式（公開資料＋Drive）Format (public accounts and Drive)

  • Drive 目標：約 3 分鐘；用故事扣入境處相關價值，勿 100% 只講個人。Drive target: about 3 minutes. Use a story to hit a value relevant to ImmD. Do not talk only about yourself.
  • 新聞／培訓機構：小組討論後即席演講；或抽英文詞、零準備、約 2.5–3 分鐘——以當日指示為準。News and training providers: the talk follows the group discussion; or an English word is drawn, with no preparation, for about 2.5–3 minutes. Follow the instructions on the day.
  • 公開資料未明確Not clearly published 官方招聘頁未必刊準確秒數。The official recruitment page may not state the exact number of seconds.

時間節奏（Drive）Timing (Drive)

  • 建議主體約 45–60 秒／故事；整段約 3 分鐘。Aim for about 45–60 seconds per story, and about 3 minutes in all.
  • 常用結構：2 個個人故事大綱 + 1 個社會／部門例子，清楚扣題（價值詞）。A usual shape: two personal outlines plus one public or departmental example, clearly tied to the value word.

故事大綱 A — 小組專題Story outline A — the group project

Drive 大綱

開場：大學最後一年帶大型小組專題，deadline 極緊，核心成員突然生病，團隊士氣低落。
轉折：Teamwork／Leadership — 重開會、按強項分工、鼓勵隊員；真正團隊在困難時互相支援。
Stress — 拆細任務、一次一題；冷靜理性對 IO 重要。
Communication — 先聽各方再決策；積極聆聽達共識。

English outline

Opening: In my final year I led a large group project. The deadline was extremely tight, a core member suddenly fell ill, and morale dropped.
Turn: Teamwork / Leadership — we met again, split work by strengths, and encouraged each other; a real team supports one another under pressure.
Stress — break the work into smaller tasks, one issue at a time; calm reasoning matters for an IO.
Communication — listen to every side before deciding; active listening builds consensus.

故事大綱 B — 兼職Story outline B — the part-time job

Drive 大綱

開場：繁忙餐廳兼職，高峰時顧客因延遲當眾喝罵。
EQ／Patience／Service — 深呼吸、專業、聆聽道歉並迅速處理。
Rules／Integrity — 不合理折扣要求下仍守政策。
Adaptability — 同時應付怒客與廚房人手不足。

English outline

Opening: I worked part-time in a busy restaurant. At peak a customer shouted at me in public because of a delay.
EQ / Patience / Service — breathe, stay professional, listen, apologise, and fix it quickly.
Rules / Integrity — when asked for an unreasonable discount, still follow the policy.
Adaptability — handle an angry customer and a short-staffed kitchen at the same time.

故事大綱 C — COVID／科技（社會）Story outline C — COVID / technology (public)

Drive 大綱

開場：疫情同時打在邊境和日常生活上。熟悉的通關方式突然不能用，隊伍、檢疫和證件查核都要在幾週內改做法。
轉折：Technology — 入境處推出無接觸 e-Channel 一類措施，讓合資格的人少接觸、仍能核對身份。科技是應變工具，不是取消檢查。
Change／Adaptability — 口罩、在家工作、流程一日一變；能留下的人是願意改步驟、而不放棄目的的人。
Health／Security — 公共衛生風險和邊境風險疊在一起，弱勢旅客更需要有人看顧，不能只顧速度。
Cooperation／Rules — 檢疫要跨部門；嚴格執行是秩序的底，而不是另加的態度。
扣到 IO：管制站每天仍會遇到突然改規則的情況。這個社會例子用來說明：適應是改方法，不是改原則。

English outline

Opening: The pandemic hit the border and daily life at the same time. Familiar clearance steps suddenly could not be used. Queues, quarantine and document checks had to change within weeks.
Turn: Technology — ImmD introduced contactless e-Channel and similar measures, so eligible people had less contact while identity was still checked. The tool answered the disruption. It did not cancel the check.
Change / Adaptability — masks, working from home, instructions that changed by the day. People got through by changing the method, not the purpose.
Health / Security — public-health risk and border risk arrived together. Elderly passengers and others who needed help could not be told only to move faster.
Cooperation / Rules — quarantine crossed departments. Firm enforcement was the floor of order, not an optional tone.
Link to an IO: A control point still meets rules that change overnight. This public example shows that adaptability means changing the method, not the principle.

完整 3 分鐘結構示例（主題：Adaptability）A full 3-minute structure (topic: Adaptability)

時間軸仍是 Drive 的四段。下面把每一段寫成可以開口的句子，深度對齊故事 A／B，而不是只有標題。The timeline is still Drive’s four blocks. Each block below is written as sentences you can say, at the same depth as stories A and B, not as headings only.

中文骨架

0:00–0:40。我把 Adaptability 定義成：情況變了，仍然完成原來的責任。它不是見風轉舵。在公共服務裡，規則和人流都會突然變，不能變的是安全和依法。

0:40–1:30，故事 B。餐廳高峰，顧客因為延遲當眾喝罵，廚房又缺人。我先讓場面停下來，聽完要求，能做的立刻做，不能做的折扣就照規定拒絕。這件事教我：臨時判斷要快，但快不等於放棄規則。

1:30–2:20，故事 C。同一種能力放到社會：疫情打亂邊境，無接觸 e-Channel 這一類措施讓合資格的人仍能通關。城市要改流程，主任要改步驟，檢查本身還在。中間用一句過橋：「個人的應變如果不能放到公眾場合，就只是求生，不是公共服務。」

2:20–3:00。管制站不會有兩天完全相同的隊伍。我準備好改方法：多一種語言、多一個分流、多一次請示。我不準備改的是身份核實和對人有禮。最後謝謝考官。

English skeleton

0:00–0:40. I define adaptability as finishing the same duty after the situation has changed. It is not following the crowd. In public service the rules and the crowd can change suddenly. Safety and the law do not.

0:40–1:30, story B. At the restaurant peak a customer shouted about a delay, and the kitchen was short of people. I paused the scene, listened, did at once what I could, and refused a discount the rules did not allow. Speed was necessary. Dropping the rule was not.

1:30–2:20, story C. The same skill at city scale: the pandemic broke the usual border routine, and contactless e-Channel let eligible people pass while identity was still checked. The city changed the process. The check remained. Bridge line: personal survival becomes public service only when it still works in front of other people.

2:20–3:00. No two days at a control point are the same queue. I will change the method — another language, another stream, another request for advice. I will not change identity checking, or courtesy. Thank you.

Action Plan（Drive）Action plan (Drive)

  1. 預先準備 3 個故事骨架，對應不同價值詞。Prepare three story skeletons in advance, for different value words.
  2. 練習自信句：Practise the confidence line: 這讓我明白，自信來自準備……This taught me that confidence comes from preparation…
  3. 控時：講太快則在故事中段加細節（顧客表情、隊友情緒）作 padding。If you are too fast, add a detail in the middle of the story (the customer’s face, a teammate’s mood) to fill the time.

禁忌（網上攻略共識）What to avoid (public guides agree)

  • 抽到不懂就改念預背「誠信講稿」答非所問If you do not know the word, do not switch to a memorised “integrity” script that does not answer it
  • 重複剛完成的小組討論內容Do not repeat the group discussion you have just finished
  • 長時間 cold airDo not leave a long silence
