卷一秒殺技巧（Drive IMMD_Paper1_MCQ_Hacks）Paper I shortcuts (Drive IMMD Paper 1 MCQ Hacks)

英語 (A) Comprehension — 定位法＋極端詞排除English (A) Comprehension — locate, then drop extreme words

  • 先讀題Read the question first：圈專有名詞、數字、獨特動詞／名詞（Keyword Scanning）。: circle names, numbers and distinctive verbs or nouns (keyword scanning).
  • 首尾句定位First and last sentences：主旨／態度題優先看首段、尾段、每段首尾句（演繹式公務文）。: for gist or attitude, read the opening, the close, and the first and last sentence of each paragraph.
  • 極端詞排除Drop extreme words：always / never / all / none / only / must — 除非原文 100% 絕對陳述，否則多數錯；優先 may / some / generally / likely。: always / never / all / none / only / must are usually wrong unless the passage is absolute. Prefer may / some / generally / likely.

英語 (B) Cloze — 前後夾擊＋邏輯連接English (B) Cloze — both sides of the blank, then the link

  • 看空格左（介詞搭配，如 conduce to）與右（名詞／動詞決定詞性）。Look left of the blank (a preposition pairing, such as conduce to) and right (a noun or verb decides the part of speech).
  • 句首／句間：相反→ However / Despite；遞進因果→ Furthermore / Therefore。Between sentences: contrast → However / Despite; addition or result → Furthermore / Therefore.

自擬Drafted Officers must act in accordance ____ the conditions stamped on the landing slip. 左面是 accordance，固定搭配是 with，不是 to。下一句若以 The passenger, ____, refused to wait. 轉折，空格是 however，不是 furthermore。Officers must act in accordance ____ the conditions stamped on the landing slip. To the left is “accordance”; the fixed pairing is with, not “to”. If the next sentence turns — The passenger, ____, refused to wait — the blank is however, not “furthermore”.

中文 (E)(F) — 成語語境＋偏旁形聲Chinese (E)(F) — idiom context and look-alike characters

官方樣只示範形式。下面是自擬對照，用來單獨複習，不是試題原文。

  • 「精神亦從此 ______」→ 無法再振作 → 一蹶不振。附近的「一敗塗地」寫的是局勢，「一落千丈」寫的是程度。
  • 聞雞起舞（開始練功）。錯字常寫成喜舞、喜武、起武。
  • 事情還有辦法，只是一時想不到 → 一籌莫展。錯字「一愁莫展」把「籌」寫成情緒。
  • 依程序、不跳步 → 按部就班。錯字「按步就班」。
  • 急到等不及 → 迫不及待。錯字「迫不急待」。
  • 消息傳得快、沒有腿也能走 → 不脛而走。東西不見了才是「不翼而飛」。兩句不要互填。
  • 一次失敗後繼續按正確方法做 → 再接再厲。越做越過分才是「變本加厲」。

The official sample only shows the format. The pairs below are drafted for revision. They are not the text of an official item. The idioms stay in Chinese because that is what the paper tests.

  • “His spirits ______ from then on” → unable to pick himself up → 一蹶不振. Nearby, 一敗塗地 describes a situation collapsing, and 一落千丈 describes a sharp drop in degree.
  • 聞雞起舞 means getting up to practise when the cock crows. Common wrong characters swap 起 for 喜, or 舞 for 武.
  • There is still a way, but none comes to mind for the moment → 一籌莫展. The wrong form 一愁莫展 writes 籌 (a plan) as the character for worry.
  • Follow the procedure and do not skip a step → 按部就班. The wrong form 按步就班 writes 部 as 步.
  • Too urgent to wait → 迫不及待. The wrong form 迫不急待 writes 及 as 急.
  • News travels fast, as if it had no legs → 不脛而走. Something that has vanished is 不翼而飛. Do not fill one blank with the other.
  • After one failure, continue by the right method → 再接再厲. Becoming more excessive is 變本加厲.

中文 (G) 語病 — 主幹提取＋雙重否定／兩面詞Chinese (G) — sentence errors

  • 劃掉定語，只留主語、謂語、賓語。搭配不當就露出來。
  • 兩面詞要對兩面：「能否成功，在於是否努力」。只寫「能否成功，在於努力」就少了一面。
  • Cross out the modifiers and keep subject, verb and object. A bad collocation shows up.
  • A two-sided word needs two sides: “whether it succeeds depends on whether the effort is there”. “Whether it succeeds depends on effort” drops one side.

自擬　錯 → 對Drafted　wrong → right

  • 警方將舉行一項措施。 → 舉行的是活動；措施用推行。
  • 必須避免不再逾期逗留。 → 「避免」已經是否定，再加「不再」會變成鼓勵逾期。改為「避免逾期逗留」或「不再逾期逗留」。
  • 能否獲准延期，在於文件齊備。 → 改為「能否獲准延期，在於文件是否齊備」。
  • 通過這次行動，使非法勞工受到打擊。 → 缺主語。改為「這次行動打擊了非法勞工」或「部門通過這次行動打擊非法勞工」。
  • Wrong: the police will hold (舉行) a measure. 舉行 goes with an event. A measure uses 推行 (carry out).
  • Wrong: we must avoid no longer overstaying. 避免 is already negative, so 不再 turns it into encouragement to overstay. Use “avoid overstaying” or “no longer overstay”, not both.
  • Wrong: whether an extension is granted depends on the documents being complete. Right: whether an extension is granted depends on whether the documents are complete.
  • Wrong: through this operation, caused illegal workers to be hit. The subject is missing. Right: “this operation hit illegal employment” or “the department, through this operation, hit illegal employment”.

能力傾向 (H) Verbal — 箭頭／三段論畫圈Aptitude (H) Verbal — arrows and circles

  • A→B 等價 ~B→~A；否前或肯後不能亂推。A→B is equivalent to not-B→not-A. Denying the antecedent or affirming the consequent does not follow.
  • All A are B／Some B are C：畫圈，勿憑空想像「有些 A 必然是 C」。All A are B / some B are C: draw the circles. Do not invent “some A must be C”.
  • 選 True / False / Can’t tell：只根據段落，勿加世界知識。True / False / Can’t tell: use only the passage. Do not add outside knowledge.

自擬Drafted 前提：所有持 A 符號的人都有居留權。某人有居留權。不能推出他持 A 符號（肯後）。前提：若落地條件是訪客，則不得受僱。某人不是訪客。不能推出他可以受僱（否前；他仍可能是學生或外傭）。Premiss: everyone with symbol A has the right of abode. Someone has the right of abode. You cannot infer that he holds symbol A (affirming the consequent). Premiss: if the landing condition is Visitor, he may not be employed. Someone is not a visitor. You cannot infer that he may be employed (denying the antecedent; he may still be a student or a domestic helper).

能力傾向 (I) Data Sufficiency — 不計算原則＋決策樹Aptitude (I) Data sufficiency — stop once the equation is enough

  1. 單獨 (1) 夠？→ 是則看 (2)：兩皆夠＝D；僅 (1)＝AIs (1) alone enough? If yes, look at (2): both enough = D; only (1) = A
  2. (1) 不夠再看 (2)：僅 (2)＝B；兩皆不夠則聯立→夠＝C／不夠＝EIf (1) is not enough, look at (2): only (2) = B; if neither is enough, together enough = C / not enough = E
  3. 夠列方程即可停，勿算盡具體數值。Stop once you can write the equation. Do not finish the arithmetic.

自擬Drafted 問：某櫃檯上午平均每小時處理多少人？ (1) 上午 4 小時共 480 人。單獨已夠列式，不必先算出 120。 (2) 下午人流是上午的 1.5 倍。(2) 沒有上午基數，單獨不夠。所以是「僅 (1) 夠」那一類。若改問全日總人數，(1) 和 (2) 各自都不夠、合在一起才夠，那才是聯立的一類。Question: how many people per hour does a counter handle in the morning, on average? (1) 480 people in 4 morning hours. That alone is enough to set up the equation; you need not first compute 120. (2) The afternoon flow is 1.5 times the morning. (2) has no morning base, so it is not enough alone. This is the “only (1) is sufficient” type. If the question were the full-day total, (1) and (2) would each be insufficient and sufficient only together.

能力傾向 (J) 圖表 — 單位／分母／尾數估算Aptitude (J) Charts — unit, denominator, last digits

  • 先圈單位（thousands / %）。Circle the unit first (thousands / %).
  • 增長率分母＝舊期（compared to 後面的年份）。The growth denominator is the earlier period (the year after “compared to”).
  • 選項個位不同→只算個位；差距大→四捨五入前兩位。If options differ in the units digit, compute only that digit. If they are far apart, round to the first two digits.

自擬Drafted 表上 2024 年為 200（單位：千人次），2025 年為 250。增長率是 (250−200)/200 = 25%，分母是 2024，不是 2025。若單位沒有看成「千」，就會把 50 當成 50 人。選項若是 20%、25%、33%，只需判斷分母是哪一年。The table shows 200 for 2024 (unit: thousands of passenger trips) and 250 for 2025. Growth is (250−200)/200 = 25%. The denominator is 2024, not 2025. If you miss the unit “thousands”, 50 looks like 50 people. If the options are 20%, 25% and 33%, you only need to judge which year is the denominator.
