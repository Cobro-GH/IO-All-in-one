用語庫／句式庫

小組討論、即席演講、面試通用。宜改成自己的語氣，避免整段背誦感。

小組討論 — 開場／推進

不如我哋先用一分鐘定義題目關鍵詞，再各自講重點，最後留時間總結。
Shall we define the key term first, share main points, then reserve time to conclude?
建基於剛才一位組員嘅觀點，我想補充兩個角度：可行性同風險。
Building on that point, I would add feasibility and risk.
我哋似乎有兩個方向，不如訂一個共同標準再比較。
We seem to have two directions—can we agree on a shared criterion before comparing?

小組討論 — 分歧／總結

我理解你嘅關注係保安；我嘅關注係旅客體驗。兩者可以靠分流同風險管理一齊做。
You emphasise security; I emphasise passenger experience. Both can be achieved via triage and risk-based checks.
總結目前共識係……尚待討論嘅係……建議下一步……
To summarise our consensus… Open points are… Next, I suggest…

即席演講 — 開場／收結

The word I have is ____. I will define it, give two reasons with examples, and conclude with its relevance to public service.
If I understand this word as ____, a practical way to look at it is…
In short, ____ reminds us to stay principled, adaptable, and accountable.

面試 — 結構語

我分三點答：原則、具體步驟、同之後點跟進。
I will answer in three parts: principles, actions, and follow-up.
喺呢個情況，我會把人身安全放喺第一位，然後先控制場面，再按程序處理。
I would prioritise safety, contain the situation, then follow procedures.
如果我唔肯定個別條文細節，我會查閱現行指引同請示上級，而唔會憑記憶硬套。
If unsure about a specific rule, I would check the current guidelines and seek advice rather than guess.

面試 — 動機／誠信

我選擇入境處，係因為工作結合把關同服務市民，而且講求細心同抗壓。
I chose ImmD because the work combines border control with public service, requiring care under pressure.
誠信對我嚟講係：即使無人看見，都按規矩同良心做事。
Integrity means doing the right thing even when no one is watching.

筆試寫作 — 連接詞（中英）

  • 首先／其次／再者／綜上所述 — First / Furthermore / However / In conclusion
  • 就持份者而言 — From a stakeholder perspective
  • 短期可見／中長期 — In the short term / over the medium term
  • 在不削弱把關能力的前提下 — Without compromising immigration control

Drive Note V3 — 面試總句

入境事務處既是執法部門，也是公共服務部門；主任須依法辦事，同時提供專業、以民為本的服務。
ImmD is both a law-enforcement and public-service department; officers must act according to law while serving the public professionally.
便利與管制不是矛盾：科技提升效率，但身份核實與風險評估仍是前提。
Facilitation and control are not contradictory: technology raises efficiency, but identity verification and risk assessment remain essential.
我會用 GSMEAC：先看 Ground 與 Situation，確立 Mission，再 Execution、Administration，最後 Command and Communication。
I would apply GSMEAC: Ground and Situation first, then Mission, Execution, Administration, and Command and Communication.
